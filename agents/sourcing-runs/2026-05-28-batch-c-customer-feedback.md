## Key territory-specific learnings (to write to memory)

1. **Customer Feedback / VoC consolidation accelerated 2023-2025.** Three rollup waves: Contentsquare (Hotjar/Heap/Loris), Qualtrics (Forsta/InMoment/Press Ganey/Decipher Oct 2025), Cisco (Slido/Smartlook). Plus opportunistic acquirers: Productboard absorbed SatisMeter, Drip absorbed Sleeknote, ABC Fitness absorbed Glofox, Norstat absorbed TestingTime, Banzai LOI'd Boast.io Jan 2024.
2. **VC-bloated mid-tier blocks the $5-15M ARR range** with companies like Beamer, Wooclap, Userlytics, Chameleon, Maze, Sprig, Outgrow that are bootstrapped-flavored but too large for $30K AI assessment.
3. **AI-native rebrand wave from 2024-2025** is heavy in feedback — Kraftful, Notably, Productlane, Markup-now-Markup-AI, Olvy, Idiomatic, Chattermill, Birdie. DQ on first pass.
4. **EU/Bratislava+Tallinn+Munich cluster** is the cleanest source of independent, bootstrapped, ICP-fit user research / survey SaaS: Condens (Munich), UXtweak (Bratislava), Survalyzer (Zurich), Survio (Brno), Userbrain (Graz), Riddle (Germany), Feedier (Lille), Feedbackly (Jyväskylä).
5. **Pointerpro / Survey Anyplace pattern** — older 2010-2014 founders, bootstrapped through pandemic, hit $3-5M ARR with 25-30 emp, frequently appear on Practical Founders / Starter Story / GetLatka. Highest-density vein.
6. **Audience-engagement / polling sub-vertical** (AhaSlides, Vevox, Wooclap, Mentimeter, Slido, Poll Everywhere, Riddle) is bigger than expected and has 2-3 cleanly bootstrapped survivors below the ICP ceiling.
7. **Solo founder skip rule** (per existing memory) costs 8-12 otherwise-fitting prospects in this territory. Worth reconsidering: founders like JR Wurster (SurveyPlanet, $1-5M ARR with 2 emp) are pulling clear $1M+ ARR and exposed to AI disruption.
