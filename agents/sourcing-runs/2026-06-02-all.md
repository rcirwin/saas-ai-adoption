## Source/category learnings
- **Agtech (farm/ranch management)** is a genuinely under-sampled, healthy bootstrapped vein. The category compresses fast above $5M (Cropin, Apify-like growth) or rebrands AI-native (Trellis→BlueCircle.ai), but $1.5-3.5M bootstrapped indies persist: FarmQA (US), iRancho (Brazil), AgriDigital (AU, but VC). Brazil agtech cluster (iRancho) is fresh — Banco do Brasil CVC ecosystem.
- **GetLatka and Tracxn now 403 to WebFetch** (new this run — previously only AppSumo/PH blocked). Per-company WebSearch verification is the workhorse now; GetLatka snippet still surfaces in WebSearch results so revenue/team snapshots remain accessible indirectly.
- **Pet boarding/kennel** (vs grooming) has clean indies: KennelBooker (Dublin, no funding). Grooming side is VC-heavy (MoeGo). ProPet/Paw Partner already in pool.
- **Tour & activity booking** consolidates hard (Resova→Clubspeed, FareHarbor→Booking Holdings). Survivors are intentionally-bootstrapped independents (Rezgo since 2005, Bookeo since 2010) — both female-founded, worth flagging.
- **Trucking SMB TMS** has true bootstrappers (Dr Dispatch since 1993) alongside VC (Truckbase). Run acquisition + funding check first.
- **Equipment rental** mostly large/PE (Point of Rental, Texada, EZO $29M, TSD $19M) — HireHop is the rare affordable UK indie.
- **Catering** is small (15 companies, $59M combined) and mostly modest — CaterZen is a clean founder-led indie.
