## Methodology notes (for memory)

- **Job-board generic searches returned meta-content, not specific company postings.** More efficient: search for known small SaaS in target categories + check whether they have AI hiring signals via founder LinkedIn or recent product announcements.
- **Disqualification rule #6 (raise >$10M with ARR <$5M)** triggered most of the skips this run — the food B2B marketplace category in particular is dominated by Series B+ funded players. Bootstrap and small Series A players are the sweet spot.
- **GetLatka remains the best ARR + employee dual-signal source.** Especially for bootstrapped firms not in Crunchbase.
- **Founder-led companies with public AI feature launches in 2024-25 are the highest-intent candidates** — e.g. Springbot's April 2025 relaunch, Spendflo's Flo AI launch, WizCommerce's WizAI engine. Even if some exceed ICP on size, the AI strategy posture is the buy signal.
