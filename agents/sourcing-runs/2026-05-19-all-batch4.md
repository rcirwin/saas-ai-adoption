## Observations
- Legal tech remains the most consolidated vertical (ProfitSolv/AffiniPay/Paradigm/CARET rollups);
  LeanLaw is one of the few still-independent survivors.
- Govtech yielded a clean cluster of bootstrapped sub-$5M ICP fits (GovPilot, Citizenserve) but
  larger players (OpenGov $128M, GovOS 176 emp) and the DQ-rule-#6 case (Govly) are common.
- Events tech is almost entirely consolidated above ICP or acquired — skip in future runs.
- Construction field/estimating tech has true bootstrappers (SiteMax, Contractor Foreman,
  Clear Estimates) but also heavy VC (Fieldwire/Hilti, FieldPulse $79M, Buildxact $33M).
- GetLatka stale-snapshot trap not hit this run, but SiteMax had conflicting figures
  ($3.1M GetLatka vs $5.3M RocketReach) — used the lower/more-recent GetLatka number.
