## Observations for memory

- **Vertical practice-management SaaS** (tutoring, fitness, real estate, moving, pest control, art galleries) is a deep, under-AI-ified vein of bootstrapped founder-led companies. Almost every category has 1-2 true independents at $1-5M ARR.
- **Manufacturing ERP for SMBs is consolidated or funded** — MRPeasy is a clean fit but Cetec crossed the cap, Digit is funded + AI-native. Low yield per search.
- **Nonprofit ops is heavily rolled up** — Bloomerang (acquisitions), Keela (ASG/Aplos), most consolidated. Little Green Light is a rare surviving independent.
- **Veterinary practice management is fully funded/consolidated** — every candidate either over cap (Shepherd) or heavily VC-backed (Digitail). Drop from regular sampling.
- **GetLatka industry indexes + per-company name verification** remains the fastest path. Always check the *most recent* revenue year — Cetec's stale $768K figure would have been a misqualification.
