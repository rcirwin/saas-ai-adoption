## Learnings for MEMORY.md
- Hospitality PMS is fully consolidated above ICP — skip this category in future runs.
- "AI-native ATS" / "AI-first" recruiting positioning is now common — fast DQ signal.
- Practical Founders podcast guest archive remains the highest-yield ICP-aligned source
  (surfaced BoosterHub + InsuredMine this run).
- Generic discovery searches ("bootstrapped SaaS for X") return mostly listicles and yield
  little — naming specific companies then verifying per-company is far more efficient.
