## Learnings for MEMORY
- GetLatka country-page ARR is capped at $5M display — several "$5M" cards were actually $7-43M (Analyse2 $42.9M, Prefixbox $8M, Swat.io €7.3M, Good Sign $10.5M). ALWAYS cross-check the most-recent-year revenue via per-company search before adding any card showing exactly $5M.
- GetLatka WebFetch pagination (`&page=2`) does NOT work — only top 10 per cap tier is fetchable. Use multiple countries/cap tiers instead.
- Croatia + Greece + Latvia + Slovakia mid/small tiers are FRESH, high-yield bootstrapped veins (data/analytics/vertical-ops). Nordics mid-cap is heavily acquired/PE-rolled-up at the top.
- Append-only rule cost 3 verified LinkedIn URLs (prezly/portal365/rentlio) — verify founder BEFORE the append next time so the row carries the URL.
