## Key learnings for memory

1. **Acquisition velocity is extreme** in SMB finance SaaS 2023-2025 — automatic "[name] acquired" search before any qualification saved ~30% of evaluation time
2. **AI-native rebrand is fastest DQ signal** — if a product page leads with "AI-powered" rather than "for accountants" it's almost always past our window
3. **Founder-built-from-frustration story** is the strongest ICP signal — every Tier 1 candidate (Hurdlr, Invoiced, MinuteDock, Hiveage, Workamajig, ProcurementExpress, ProfitBooks, AccountsPortal) was built by a founder who was solving their own pain
4. **GetLatka snapshot accuracy improved** but still stale on Saasu/Mango/Datamolino — cross-verify via Crunchbase/Tracxn
5. **Time tracking is the largest bootstrapped vein** in finance-adjacent SaaS — Memtime, Avaza, actiTIME, WebWork, TimeKeeper, MinuteDock all met Tier 1 criteria, plus Hubstaff (too large) and CAKE.com (too large)
6. **Multi-product parents under bootstrapped wrapper** (Saaslogic-Expeed, Striven-Miles, MyHours-Spica) — apply parent rule strictly
7. **Receipt/OCR niche has rapid AI-positioning consolidation** — Parseur, Datamolino, Tabscanner, BillBjorn, DocuClipper still hold pre-AI bootstrapped positioning; SparkReceipt, Tofu, Receipt-AI rebranded to AI-native
8. **PE rollups in vertical accounting are systematic** — ProfitSolv, EverCommerce, Visma, IRIS Software, ASG, Mainsail Partners, Volaris each acquired 3-5 accountant-tech products in 2018-2024; assume any "established" practice management tool >$10M ARR is owned
