## Key learnings for memory

1. **Atlassian Marketplace is heavily PE-rolled-up via Appfire** ($100M TA Associates + Silversmith investment). Appfire owns: Botron, Wittified, Bob Swift Atlassian Apps, BigPicture/SoftwarePlant, Comalatech, Spartez, Anova Apps, Feed Three. Always check "[vendor] Appfire" before qualifying any Atlassian marketplace candidate.
2. **Tempo Software also a rollup**: Acquired ALM Works (2021), Old Street Solutions, Amovos. Plus Powerful integrations now part of Tempo platform.
3. **GitKraken acquired BigBrassBand** (Git Integration for Jira).
4. **WordPress plugin ecosystem dominated by Awesome Motive / WPBeginner Growth Fund / StellarWP / Liquid Web**. WPMU DEV (IncSub), WPBeakery/Themeforest by Envato. True indie WP shops are rare survivors: Saturday Drive, WPManageNinja, WP Fusion, Brainstorm Force (large but private).
5. **Vietnamese Shopify app ecosystem is rich**: OmegaTheme (since 2015), Avada, GemPages, PageFly, Beae, Globo, Joy. All bootstrapped or lightly funded. Common pattern: 15-100 emp, 1-10M ARR range, $10-99/mo SaaS pricing on Shopify.
6. **Bangladesh WP shop**: WPManageNinja (Shahjahan Jewel) is a productive vein — Fluent Forms, FluentCRM, FluentBoards, FluentCart, FluentPlayer all from same parent. 115 emp.
7. **GetLatka stale-snapshot pattern**: Plauti latest GetLatka snapshot ($1M ARR) doesn't reflect their Aug 2024 acquisition of Super Round Robin or current growth.
8. **AI positioning is a strong DQ signal**: Relume, Slater, Anima, Synder, "Squivr AI", BoosterAI all DQ'd. Slater specifically because Edgar Allan rebranded it as "Powered by AI" — formerly a clean fit.
9. **Founder-led + small angel round is OK**: Common Ninja $1.2M raise, Loop Subscriptions Klaviyo angel — these passed even though strict bootstrapping rule would skip them. Distinguish from Bunny.net ($5.88M Series A) which crossed line.
10. **Female-founded Atlassian vendors are extremely rare**: Highway Three (Karen Fishwick) noted as unique positioning angle.
11. **Family-owned + multi-decade WP shops underpriced**: FORSBERG+two (Bjorn + wife Sarah, Copenhagen since 2010), Saturday Drive (Kevin Stover + James Laws Tennessee), Marsello (Brent Spicer NZ since 2014).
12. **Two main bootstrapped Atlassian clusters surfaced**: Eastern European (codefortynine Germany, HeroCoders Poland, SaaSJet Ukraine, Stiltsoft Estonia, eazyBI Latvia, SoftComply Estonia, Caelor Croatia, Mibex Switzerland, Plerdy Ukraine) + Antipodean (Easy Agile Australia, ProjectBalm Australia, Aprika Australia, Highway Three Canada).
