## Thesis

Y Combinator batches W15–S20 graduated cohorts that are now 5–11 years post-batch. The successful ones have IPO'd or hit unicorn status (Brex, Rippling, Gusto, Lattice, Vanta, Substack, etc.). The middle tier — the survivors that hit $1–5M ARR and plateaued — are uniquely positioned for FRS's offer:

- Real users, real workflows, real revenue
- Decision-makers (founders) still in the seat
- Many shipped before the GPT-3/4 era and haven't reorganized around AI
- Most are sub-$5M ARR with 5–50 employees → perfectly aligned to ICP
- Several show signs of "AI-vulnerable workflow": their product abstracts a human-intensive workflow (calendar, project tracking, manual ops, data wrangling, sales calls) that AI agents will increasingly disintermediate

## Patterns observed

1. **Acquisition rate is extreme**: roughly 35-45% of W15-S18 B2B SaaS got acquired by larger players. This narrowed the candidate pool aggressively.

2. **The S20 batch is the richest source** — it's still early enough that companies haven't been swept up, but they're 5+ years in and have real revenue. 10 of 30 prospects are from S20.

3. **"AI-powered" marketing is now table stakes** — many of our targets (Sunsama, Slite, Taskade, Cosmic, Gmelius) have rebranded with "AI" but their core workflows are pre-LLM. **This is exactly our wedge**: they need a strategic AI blueprint, not just feature additions. These are *prime* FRS prospects.

4. **Bootstrapped + YC + plateaued = ideal**: NetBeez, Apozy, Worklytics, OpenWrench, Names & Faces, Thematic, BaseDash all bootstrapped post-YC seed. Decision-maker is the founder. Sub-$5M ARR. Workflow-heavy. **These are the highest-conviction prospects.**

5. **Vertical SaaS within W15-S20 batches plateaued more than horizontal SaaS** — Canix (cannabis), Dripos (coffee shops), OpenWrench (restaurants), OpenUnit (storage), Sourcify (manufacturing). They have specialized customers and workflows that AI agents will eventually attack vertically.

## Notes for researcher

- For each prospect, the researcher should verify: (a) ARR estimate from GetLatka or current job postings, (b) latest AI marketing/feature posture from homepage in last 30 days, (c) founder LinkedIn activity in last 90 days.
- **Cosmic, Slite, Sunsama, Taskade, Gmelius** all show signs of "we're adding AI but unclear strategy" — these are the highest-conviction outreach targets. Lead with the "you've added AI features; now structure your product as agent-ready" angle.
- **Apozy, NetBeez, OpenWrench, Names & Faces, Thematic, Worklytics, BaseDash** are bootstrapped — they have minimum resources for an AI bet, making the FRS $30k assessment a high-leverage spend.
- **Heron Data, Datafold, Paragon, Seam, Mozart Data** are technical/devtools — researcher should adopt a technical posture in outreach (workflow disruption thesis, agent-callable APIs).
