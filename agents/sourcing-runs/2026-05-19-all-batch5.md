## Key learnings

1. **Vertical practice-management SaaS for niche professions is the richest under-mined vein** — equine, funeral homes, optometry, dental, speech therapy, chiropractic, hospice all produced clean bootstrapped sub-$5M ICP fits. Most are founder-built ("built by a dentist / PT / hospice pro for their peers").
2. **Govtech beyond municipal-ERP is largely consolidated** — iWorQ ($13.7M), GeoCivix (CivicPlus), GovOS all over ICP or acquired. Parks-rec is the exception (MyRec.com is independently owned). Permitting/code-enforcement is fully rolled up (Tyler, OpenGov, CivicPlus).
3. **Field-service software is heavily PE/holding-company consolidated** — pool (Wise→Forterro), pest control (Fieldster→Teamfront), solar (SolarNexus→Solo), golf (Club Caddie→Jonas/Constellation), janitorial (Janitorial Manager under Double A Solutions multi-product parent). Towing (Towbook) is a rare independent bootstrapper. Always run "[name] acquired" + check for multi-product holding parents before qualifying field-service candidates.
4. **Religious/community org software has genuine bootstrapped independents** — Flocknote (ESOP, no VC), ChurchTrac (no funding, but at $8M over cap). New productive niche.
5. **GetLatka revenue snapshots conflict** — Schedulefly showed both "$660K" and "$12.6M" across the same source set. When data conflicts on a tiny-team bootstrapper, mark arr_estimate=unknown rather than guess.
6. **Solo-founder companies were DQ'd** (Yardbook, PantrySoft, Sparkie) — per established memory rule; one-person operations are too small/fragile for the $30K assessment engagement.
7. **Brewery/winery software is a healthy under-mined cluster** — Breww, BrewPlanner, VinesOS all independent bootstrappers; the big name (Ekos) is the only consolidated one.
8. **Association management (AMS) is consolidated under Community Brands** but Novi AMS and StarChapter remain independent, small-association-focused players.
