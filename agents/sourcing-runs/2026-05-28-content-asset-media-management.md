## Process notes for next run

- **Rate limit hit twice during run** at ~10-15 sheet writes per minute (60/min quota with 10 parallel sibling agents). Built retry-with-exponential-backoff into batch scripts. Going forward: pace appends with 3-5 sec delay between calls.
- **Two accidental dupes appended in same run** (usersnap after fact-checked acquired status; spotlightr-vooplayer same product as spotlightr). Going forward: before appending any candidate, do full `grep -i` dedup check against in-memory cache AND verify acquisition status with "[name] acquired" search BEFORE adding.
- **TMS/Melograno multi-product parent caught after append.** Trafft was already in prospects → Amelia, wpDataTables are same founder/parent. Future: when researching a candidate by founder name, search "[founder] other products" and check for parent overlap.
- **GetLatka, Practical Founders, Indie Hackers, Crunchbase, getmasset.com, brandlife.io, fast.io, picflow comparison pages** were workhorse discovery sources for this territory. Acquire.com listings ("360° Virtual Tour SaaS — 30K+ Users") surfaced 1-2 candidates.
- **Disqualification ratio in this territory was ~40%** (vs 60% in horizontal SaaS). Content/asset/media management is genuinely under-mined for indie/bootstrapped SaaS at the ICP target band — likely because the category is intellectually fragmented (DAM ≠ PIM ≠ headless CMS ≠ video hosting) and tooling is generally still bespoke/non-AI.
