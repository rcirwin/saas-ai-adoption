## Key Learnings (added to MEMORY.md)

1. **Vertical EdTech for healthcare CE, sales training, and HR L&D** has a high concentration of bootstrapped 5-30 employee companies sitting at $1-5M ARR. Future EdTech runs should prioritize these niches.

2. **Tutoring/music school management consolidated** under Jonas Software / Vertus Group: Teachworks, Pike13. Port 443 (TutorBird/MyMusicStaff/AthletaDesk) is rare independent multi-product parent.

3. **GetLatka stale-snapshot trap**: Boddle showed $0 raised in one snapshot but $4.35M raised in earlier press. Always cross-verify GetLatka claim with Crunchbase/press.

4. **Indian + Brazilian + Eastern European** EdTech is a fertile under-sampled vein for bootstrapped fits: Almabase (Bangalore), Mykademy, BrainCert, Learnyst, Abara (India); PhishX (Brazil); Adeption, Atiom (Singapore/NZ); LanguaTalk (Oregon/global); Culcha (Berlin); Whale (Belgium).

5. **K-12 enrollment and small private school SIS** is heavily consolidated above ICP cap (Aeries-SIS $72.5M; FACTS owned by Community Brands; Blackbaud public) but there are still founder-led indies under-the-radar: QuickSchools, Schoolable, Sycamore, DreamClass, SchoolCues, BigSIS.

6. **Library systems** remain a sleepy bootstrapped vein — LibraryWorld (6 emp since 1985) and Surpass Software (40-year independent) are both clean fits.

7. **Microlearning + corporate L&D** is at peak consolidation: every well-known player (TalentLMS, Schoox, Easygenerator) is either over ICP or VC-backed. The fits are small geographically-diverse plays: Culcha (Berlin), Coassemble (AU), Whale (BE), Adeption (NZ), Atiom (SG), Auzmor (IN $7.4M).

8. **Higher-ed advising/alumni** has fewer fits than expected. ConexED, Almabase are clean fits but Stellic, Acadeum are VC-funded; OpenApply / Hivebrite skipped as too large.
