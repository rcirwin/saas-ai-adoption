The team is being deliberately shaped into "AI conductors" and outsourcing the AI strategy/workflow research feels like outsourcing the muscle they're building. The work IS the growth opportunity for the leads they want to develop.

This is a sophisticated objection. It tells us Rain has thought about the engagement seriously enough to articulate exactly why he's not buying — and the reason isn't a shortcoming in the FRS pitch, it's a strategic decision about how they want to build internal capability.

1. **The pitch landed cleanly.** "Defining our AI workflow strategy and mapping the sequence for agent-ready features is the most exciting and critical part of our current roadmap" is almost a paraphrase of the FRS deliverables list. The diagnosis was right.
2. **Fit-5 prospects with strong product-led founders may decline for this exact reason more often than not.** When the founder is also the head of product and is deliberately growing PMs into strategic AI thinkers, the engagement collides with their team-development thesis.
3. **The sign-off "best of luck with Scale Insights" is interesting.** Either Rain noticed Scale Insights in our case studies (objections.md mentions it), or it's a coincidence. Worth noting either way — these prospects are reading the FRS materials.
