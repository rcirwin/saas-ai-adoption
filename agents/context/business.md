# Future Ready Studio — Business Context

## About

Future Ready Studio (FRS) is a boutique AI adoption consultancy that helps B2B SaaS companies integrate AI into their products and workflows. We partner with founders and product teams at the seed-to-Series B stage to identify high-leverage AI opportunities, build proof-of-concepts, and ship production-ready AI features.

## Services

- **AI Readiness Assessment** — 2-week sprint to audit workflows, data, and product surface area for AI applicability
- **AI Feature Design & Build** — Embedded delivery team that designs and ships AI-powered features (LLM, classification, summarisation, recommendation)
- **AI Workflow Automation** — Internal tooling to automate repetitive operations (support triage, document generation, lead scoring)
- **Ongoing AI Advisory** — Monthly fractional CTO/AI advisory retainer

## Ideal Client Profile (ICP)

- **Company type:** B2B SaaS (not consumer, not pure services)
- **Stage:** Seed to Series B
- **ARR:** $250K – $10M (runtime override: see `icp_arr_min` / `icp_arr_max` in config sheet)
- **Employees:** 5 – 100
- **AI maturity:** Not AI-native; has a core product that could benefit from AI augmentation
- **Pain signals:** Manual workflows, repetitive support tickets, unstructured data, no AI on roadmap yet

## Disqualification Criteria

- B2C or services/agency companies
- ARR > $50M or > 100 employees (too large for boutique engagement)
- Already AI-native (AI is the core product, not an enhancement opportunity)
- Acquired or acqui-hired

## Competitive Positioning

FRS is NOT a generic AI agency. We go deep with a small number of SaaS clients and operate as an embedded team. Engagements start at $15K and scale with scope.
