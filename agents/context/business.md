## What We Do

Future Ready Studio runs a $30,000 fixed-scope AI integration assessment for B2B SaaS companies at $500K–$5M ARR. 8–12 weeks. Four deliverables. No implementation — strategy and blueprint only.

## The Core Thesis

SaaS products built for human users will be disrupted by AI agent-native competitors. Products that aren't structured for agent discovery, interaction, and orchestration get routed around. The window to adapt is closing.

## Two Angles

1. **AI experiences for users**: Map workflow friction → design AI-powered features that act, not just suggest
2. **Agent-ready product**: Structure the product so external AI agents can discover, interact with, and orchestrate it

## Four Deliverables

1. Workflow & Pain Point Audit
2. User-Centered Insight Synthesis
3. Data Readiness & Competitive Moat Assessment
4. AI-Enhanced Experience & Agentic Blueprint

## Process

- Weeks 1–2: Intake & Discovery
- Weeks 3–5: User Research & Workflow Mapping
- Weeks 6–9: Analysis & Blueprint Development
- Weeks 10–12: Review Workshop & Final Artifacts
