## Notes on confidence

- Van der Blom Algorithm InSights Report 2025 (analyzed 1.8M posts) is the primary upstream source for most of the corrections above. Direct PDF access was rate-limited; figures pulled from secondary write-ups (WrittenlyHub, Mercer-Mackay, AuthoredUp, autoposting.ai) that all cite the same 2025 report.
- Hootsuite and AuthoredUp domains repeatedly returned 403 to direct fetch — relied on search-result summaries from those sources instead.
- Claim 10 is the only one I could not independently confirm from a primary source; recommend treating as heuristic, not data.
