## BUG FIXED THIS RUN: the departed-disqualifier regex was matching "complexity"

The acquisition half of the pre-filter regex was `sold to|acquired|exit(ed)?` with no word boundaries. `exit` is a substring of **compl-exit-y**, and "workflow complexity" appears in almost every `research_cache.pain_signals` and `fit_assessment` row. First pass disqualified **167** prospects; 132 of those were acquisition-verb hits and the overwhelming majority were this false positive.

Two `tn` candidates (`datascan`, `sessions-health`) and several parallel-email candidates (`postaga`, `swordfish-software`, `wiztopic`, `surveyplanet`) were wrongly removed. After switching to word-anchored `\b(sold to|acquired by|was acquired|acquisition of|exited|founder exit)\b` and tightening the leadership half to `\b(no longer (the )?(ceo|founder|with|at|running|leads)|stepped down|former ceo|founder[- ]departed|departed|new ceo|left the company|succeeded (as|by))\b`, the departed bucket landed at **42**, consistent with prior runs, and every other bucket returned to its expected magnitude (in_cadence 45, both_touched 508).

Genuine disqualifications retained: 23 `skipped-founder-departed` log rows, `order-printer-pro` (`contact_role` reads "Founder (sold to Shop Circle 2024)"), `employeeconnect` (Craig Macdonald no longer CEO, 5th consecutive run skipped, still not re-researched), `snap-surveys`, `deconetwork`.
