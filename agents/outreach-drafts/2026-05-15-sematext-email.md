Otis,

17 years bootstrapped, 100% virtual, 3x cheaper than Datadog while serving ASOS, WebMD, MIT, and Broadcom is one of the more impressive operator stories in observability. The reason I am writing is the Bits AI question. The audit I run with bootstrapped founders in AI-saturated technical categories is not "ship Bits AI parity." It is the workflow question. Where does AI inside Sematext actually save an SRE 6 hours a week on incident triage and root-cause, in a way that fits the lean bootstrapped operation. That is sharper than chasing Datadog's feature surface.
