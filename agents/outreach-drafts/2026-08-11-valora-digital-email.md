Subject: the part of fan engagement that didn't get cheap

Borja,

Fan-engagement tooling is converging on one demo. Generate the poll, cut the clip, summarize the sentiment. That work is now cheap for anyone with an API key, which is why every sports-tech deck looks the same this year.

The part that stayed expensive is the part you carry. Which club's data can legitimately reach which telco's subscribers, in which tenant, under which window. Seven years of running that across media, telcos, clubs and brands is a configuration and permission problem, and no general model inherits it.

I would start there rather than at the sentiment classifier, because that is where the defensible surface actually sits.

If useful, I can share the directional read on where I'd start with Valora, no commitment. Open to a short call or just trading notes here.
