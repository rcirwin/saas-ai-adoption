Subject: Discovery AI shipped, then what

Danny,

Discovery AI launching in November was a real product call, not a press release. Most behavior-analytics tools shipped an "ask questions" assistant in 2025 because Clarity made it free, but yours actually answers on live visitor behavior, which is harder than the dashboard summarizers.

The reason I am writing is what comes next. The category is at the early bolt-on phase. By Q3 2026 every behavior tool has an ask-questions assistant. Hotjar (Contentsquare's budget), FullStory (already AI-native), Mouseflow (Mina AI), Clarity (free + AI summaries). The "AI inside your analytics" feature stops differentiating in the next two quarters.

The audit I run with founders in this position is the act-not-suggest question. Where does AI inside Lucky Orange actually take action on the friction you spot, not just describe it. Auto-flagging form-abandonment cohorts to a CSV. Triggering a retargeting workflow when rage-click thresholds hit. That is workflow AI, not assistant AI, and the bootstrapped 13-year operator advantage actually shows up in that audit.
