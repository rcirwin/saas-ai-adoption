AI Voice Booking is the obvious move. Worth thinking about the less obvious one.

The category pressure isn't really Calendly. It's that personal AI assistants are starting to book on behalf of users. That's a different question than "ship more AI features on the dashboard." It's about whether SimplyBook.me is structured so external scheduling agents can find it, query availability, and book without a human ever opening the app.
