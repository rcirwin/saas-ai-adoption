Subject: what changed when status pages got the sequence right

Robin,

Something shifting in the status page category that I do not see discussed much. The pages are increasingly not read by people. Vendor risk tools, procurement questionnaires and monitoring bots pull the feed, and during an incident a customer's own assistant often summarizes the page before anyone reads it.

That inverts what the page is for. Prose written to reassure a worried customer matters less. Structured incident history that holds up when a buyer's security review asks for twelve months of uptime evidence matters more, and it is a different build from adding a summarizer.

SorryApp has a decade of incident records to work from. Most of the newer entrants have two years.
