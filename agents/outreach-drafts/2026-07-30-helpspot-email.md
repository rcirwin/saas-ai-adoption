Ian,

HelpSpot AI shipped as a hotkey inside the agent's flow rather than a bot in front of the customer. Twenty years in, that reads as a deliberate call about who stays in the seat.

Here is the tension I would name. Fin and Freddy are eating tier-one volume across the category, which means the tickets that still reach a human get harder, not easier. Assist quality on hard tickets depends almost entirely on how well twenty years of resolved threads are structured and retrievable. That is a data question before it is a model question, and it is the one most helpdesks skip because the bot demo is louder.

I run a fixed-scope assessment for B2B SaaS at $500K to $5M ARR, 8 to 12 weeks, four deliverables. It starts with workflow research, not feature ideation.
