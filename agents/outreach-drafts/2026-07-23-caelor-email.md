The November Cosmos upgrade went into personalization and admin UX, not another AI feature, which tells me you already see where the friction actually is. Configuring an intranet for a large Confluence tenant is heavy, manual work.

The question I keep landing on for tools at your stage is whether that admin burden is the workflow AI should quietly take over first, targeting the right news to the right teams, keeping structure clean, before anyone bolts a chatbot onto the front. Rovo is going to surface intranet content either way. The setup work underneath it is where a small team wins or drowns.
