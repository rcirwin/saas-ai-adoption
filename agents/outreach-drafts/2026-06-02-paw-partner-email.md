Subject: Paw Partner, built from the kennel floor up

Austin,

Building Paw Partner after running a 150-plus dog/day facility and demoing every competitor is the kind of origin that shows in the product. You know the workflow because you live it.

The thing I'd watch in your seat is the other side of that. Pet-care software is mostly manual onboarding and coordination today, and the first real AI win is rarely a chatbot. It is the boring stuff: post-stay summaries, vaccination data capture, occupancy prediction. That is a workflow question, not a feature one, and it is easy to miss when the product already works.

I run a fixed-scope assessment for B2B SaaS at $500K to $5M ARR, 8 to 12 weeks, four deliverables. It starts with workflow research, not feature ideation.

Worth a 20-minute call to compare notes on where that research would start for Paw Partner?
