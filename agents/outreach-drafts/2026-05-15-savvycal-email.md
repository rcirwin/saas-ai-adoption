Derrick,

I caught the MicroConf 2026 coverage and your Shipper experiment with Claude Opus 4.6 building native iOS apps end to end. The interesting signal in there is not the side project. It is that you are deep in Claude Code daily AND deliberately chose not to ship AI features in SavvyCal. That is a clearer read of the category than most founders working on scheduling right now.

Support triage, dunning, churn analysis, content. The audit looks different than the product one and most founders skip it because the framing is conflated.

Worth 20 minutes to compare notes on what that audit looks like for a TinySeed-shaped team? Happy to share what I am seeing with similar founders first.
