Janna,

Across PM tooling the artifact is getting cheap. Generated roadmap views, summarized feedback, drafted specs. Productboard, Aha and Canny all ship a version of it and the output converges fast.

You have argued for years that a roadmap is a statement of intent rather than a delivery schedule. That framing is what survives this. A model can produce the document. It cannot produce the reasoning about why an idea sits in later, or the record of what the team said no to and why.

CoPilot is shipped, so this is not an add AI note. The question is whether ProdPad becomes the place that holds the decision record, rejected ideas included, once every tool can render the roadmap itself.

If useful, I can share the directional read on where I'd start with ProdPad, no commitment. Open to a short call or just trading notes here.
