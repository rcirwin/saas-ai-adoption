Peter,

Doubling ARR bootstrapped while staying on the core is a real signal, so I will not tell you to bolt on AI because OptiSigns won an award.

The version of signage AI that would actually matter for TelemetryTV is not a content generator. It is a screen that reacts to context, time of day, what is nearby, who is likely watching, and picks what to show. That is a data-and-orchestration problem far more than a model problem, and it is the kind of thing that is much easier to design before the content engine is fully set than after.
