Ervin,

I have followed your revenue posts. From zero to a $3M target while almost dying twice is the kind of story that earns the right to be picky about where money goes next.

Which is why I would not tell you to out-feature Blotato on AI. They are VC-funded and AI-native, and matching them caption-for-caption burns runway you cannot spare. The sharper question is what one part of the posting workflow you would rebuild agent-first if you started today. Not 'AI Assist that suggests text' but AI that plans, drafts, and schedules a week across channels with the operator approving, not typing.

Pick that one move well and you compete on workflow, where a lean team that knows its customers can beat a funded generalist.

I run a practice that maps exactly that for bootstrapped B2B SaaS at $500K to $5M ARR.

Worth 20 minutes to compare notes on Publer specifically?
