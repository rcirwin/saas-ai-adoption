Nicholas,

Twenty-four years and 400 brands is a rare thing to build without outside money, and the financial services weighting inside that base changes which AI questions are even available to you.

Twilio and Bird can push message generation because most of their volume is not regulated. Yours is. In banking comms the expensive question is proving what was sent, to whom, on which channel, under which consent, and reconstructing it later when somebody asks. Two billion interactions a year makes that a data problem worth owning rather than a feature to bolt on, and RCS leaves a far richer record than SMS ever did.

I run a fixed-scope assessment for B2B SaaS at $500K to $5M ARR, 8 to 12 weeks, four deliverables. It starts with workflow research, not feature ideation.

Worth a 20-minute call to compare notes on where that research would start for Grapevine?
