Subject: what changed when bookkeeping stopped competing on accuracy

Francois,

Categorization accuracy was the scoreboard in bookkeeping for a decade, and OneUp won it publicly. That scoreboard is closing. Once every vendor runs a current model over the same bank feeds, accuracy converges toward one ceiling and stops being a reason to choose.

What is left is the exception. The transaction the system is unsure about, who gets asked, how fast they answer, and whether the answer teaches the system anything. Across 700,000 businesses, OneUp holds years of those corrections. A 2017 ML core is a fair criticism of the classifier. It is not a criticism of the correction history, and that history is the part QuickBooks and Xero cannot copy by shipping an agent.
