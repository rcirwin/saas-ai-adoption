Subject: The middle of the scheduling category is the squeeze zone

Bryce,

Setmore at $3.2M ARR with a 200-appointment free plan and 14 years bootstrapped is a clean SMB service-business product. Salons, dental clinics, PT, photographers. Workflow-heavy users who actually need the booking layer to be reliable, not flashy.

The reason I am writing is what is happening on both sides of you. Cal.com is open-source and free, pressuring the free plan from below. SimplyBook.me shipped AI Voice Booking. Motion and Reclaim are AI-native agents for adjacent buyers. GoodCall is shipping AI receptionist integration. The middle (simple booking with bolt-on AI) is the squeeze zone.

The audit I run with SMB-tools founders in this position is not "ship an AI receptionist." It is the workflow-first question. Salons, dental clinics, photographers each have a specific friction (no-show prediction, multi-staff calendar coordination, customer reminder timing) where AI actually moves retention. AI receptionist is a feature. Workflow AI per service vertical is the moat.

Worth 20 minutes to compare notes on what that looks like for Setmore's actual buyers?
