Subject: No backend was the bet. Where does AI fit without breaking it?

Sergey,

EmailJS won a specific bet: developers shouldn't need a backend to send email. Ten years solo and still owning that niche while Resend and the rest reposition around "AI-Powered Email" says the primitive matters more than the buzzword.

The thing worth thinking through is where AI actually fits a client-side product without breaking the thesis. Most of the AI-email noise is server-side. The developer-facing version (template generation, deliverability hints, error triage right in the JS workflow) is a different and smaller surface, and getting that scope right is the hard part for a lean team.

I run a fixed-scope assessment for B2B SaaS at $500K to $5M ARR, 8 to 12 weeks, four deliverables. It starts with workflow research on where your developers actually hit friction, not with feature ideation.
