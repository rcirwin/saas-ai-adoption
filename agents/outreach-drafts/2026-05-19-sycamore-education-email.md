Glen,

Reaching hundreds of thousands of users across five continents, and recently rebranding to Sycamore Leaf, says the platform has staying power in school management.

The reason AI is a harder question here than in most SaaS: the product serves admins, teachers, parents and students, and each one has a different workflow and a different definition of friction. "Add AI" is meaningless without first deciding whose pain you are solving. Admissions and scheduling look like the obvious candidates, but that should be tested, not assumed.

I run a practice that helps B2B SaaS teams figure out which AI features matter and which to skip. It starts with a workflow and pain-point audit across the real stakeholders, not a feature brainstorm.

Worth a 20-minute call to see if it is relevant for Sycamore?
