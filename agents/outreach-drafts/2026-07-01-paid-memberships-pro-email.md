Jason,

The membership tools getting AI right are, like PMP Max, using it on their own side first. Prompt libraries for dev, support, and marketing are a genuinely sharp internal move, and most teams skip straight to a member-facing gimmick and regret it.

The harder, unshipped question is whether the member's own experience is where AI eventually moves the ARR number. Re-engaging a lapsing member, routing them to the right content, catching a cancel before it happens. That is a retention lever, not a feature, and it is a different design problem than the internal prompt work.
