Subject: Max, your 30-person team, and the compounding question

Amar,

Your "non-technical is a limiting belief in 2026" post is the cleanest articulation I've seen of where AI shifts the solo builder. The interesting question for ZenMaid is whether the same logic compounds at 30 people.

Most founders shipping that line are running 1-to-3-person teams where every hour saved goes straight to product. At 30 staff with 3,000 maid services using the product, the math gets weirder. Some of the time-savings get absorbed by coordination. Some of it doesn't. Some of it shows up in the cleaning operator's day, not yours.

I run a fixed-scope assessment for B2B SaaS at $500K to $5M ARR. The first deliverable is workflow mapping for your customers' day. The fourth is the AI bet priority. For a vertical SaaS with the niche depth ZenMaid has, the right two bets are almost never the ones Jobber and Housecall are shipping.

Worth a 20-minute call? Curious whether Max has been working the way you expected.
