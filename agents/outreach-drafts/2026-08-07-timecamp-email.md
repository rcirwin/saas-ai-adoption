Kamil,

Time tracking accuracy has converged. Timely, Toggl and TimeCamp all classify app activity into projects well enough now that categorization is not where anyone wins. You shipped auto categorization and so did everyone else.

The layer above it is the interesting one. Project profitability and resource utilization are why an agency pays for this at all, and that math carries an assumption. It assumes hours are the unit that delivery is made of.

That assumption is slipping. When part of a project gets done by a model in four minutes, the profitability report gets more flattering and less useful at the same time. Sixteen years of tracked project data is a good place to work out what utilization should mean instead.
