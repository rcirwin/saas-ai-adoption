Antonio,

The "do one thing well" philosophy behind Hireflix reads like a deliberate stance, not just an outcome. In a category where Truffle is going AI-native and HireVue is bolting AI scoring everywhere, that stance gets more valuable, not less.

The question worth pressuring isn't whether to add AI. It's which one or two AI moves deepen the core async-video-to-hire-decision workflow without expanding surface area or compromising the simplicity bet. HireVue's AI scoring has well-documented ethical and operational problems. The space for a thoughtful, narrow AI position is open.

I work with B2B SaaS founders at $500K to $5M ARR on exactly this question. Not "what AI to bolt on" but "where AI compounds the workflow you've already chosen." Workflow-first, no implementation, just the map of which one or two moves matter and which to skip without diluting the philosophy.

Worth 20 minutes to compare notes on where AI fits in Hireflix specifically, given the philosophy?
