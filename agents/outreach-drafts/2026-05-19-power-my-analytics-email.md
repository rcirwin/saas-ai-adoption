Morgan,

Power My Analytics does the unglamorous, genuinely hard thing well: clean marketing data out of 75+ platforms, at a price that undercuts everyone. 100 signups a day says the value is real.

Two things are shifting under that. First, the data still stops at delivery. Users export to a BI tool or a spreadsheet to actually interpret it, and that interpretation step is exactly what an AI query layer removes. Second, when a marketer asks an AI agent "pull my ad spend across channels," the platform the agent can reach wins the workflow. Supermetrics and Windsor are building toward both. Competing on price alone gets harder when a competitor is also the one the agent talks to.

I run a practice that helps B2B SaaS teams figure out which AI opportunities matter and which to skip, based on real workflow research.
