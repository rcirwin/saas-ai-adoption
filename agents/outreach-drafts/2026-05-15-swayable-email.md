Subject: Swayable + causal AI in agent-mediated ad buying

James,

The causal-AI platform positioning is the right move against Vidmob, Vizit, Memorable. Worth thinking about the next layer up.

What's interesting in measurement-tech right now is that ad buying itself is starting to be mediated by agents, not just humans reading reports. A campaign-optimization agent doesn't open a Swayable dashboard. It queries the causal model directly, gets a recommendation on which creative variant to weight, and adjusts the buy. The reports Swayable produces today are designed for humans. The reports causal AI will need to produce next year are designed for other AI to act on.

That's a different kind of "agent-ready" than ship more features. It's about whether Swayable's measurement output is structured for machine consumption inside an ad-buying workflow.
