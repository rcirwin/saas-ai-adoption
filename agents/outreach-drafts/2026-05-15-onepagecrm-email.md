Subject: Action Stream methodology and where AI actually fits

Michael,

The Currency profile (opposite of fake company burning money chasing growth) and the Revenue E467 conversation made it clear OnePageCRM is a different posture than the rest of the SMB CRM category. Action Stream as a methodology, not just a feature, is the actual moat.

The reason I am writing is what is happening on the AI side. The Route Planner is a reasonable bolt-on, but the more interesting question is what AI does inside the Action Stream methodology itself. HubSpot, Pipedrive, and Salesforce are racing to add AI assistants to their CRM tables. OnePageCRM does not have a CRM table problem. You have an Action Stream that nobody else has.

The audit I run with bootstrapped founders in this position is the methodology-deepening question. Where does AI inside OnePageCRM make Action Stream measurably better at "next action" decisions. Decay scoring on stale contacts. Recommended next-action based on prior similar deals. Cross-customer pattern surfacing. That is product strategy work that compounds the moat instead of chasing HubSpot.
