Subject: Scribe is shipped, what's the next layer

Darren,

The "Embracing AI for A+ Patient Care" framing on the blog and the "Automate Your Clinic" webinar this quarter is a clearer AI posture than most allied-health practice mgmt vendors have published. Clinical AI scribe shipped, with playback and template-fill on top, is real product work.

The reason I am writing is the shape of the next 18 months. Cliniko published their own AI stance, Halaxy claims 700 clinical AI tools, Carepatron rebranded AI-first. Scribe is becoming table stakes by Q3 2026. The differentiation moves to the workflow primitives Nookal actually owns: scheduling triage, billing exception review, clinical pathway adherence across multidisciplinary clinics, intake routing.

The audit I run with vertical SaaS founders who have shipped early AI is the "what's the layer above" question. Where does AI inside Nookal save a 5-clinic physiotherapy group 12 hours a week on the non-clinical operating load. That answer is workflow research, not feature roadmap.

Worth 20 minutes to compare notes on what that looks like for Nookal specifically?
