Filip,

Whova and Cvent are bolting AI matchmaking onto event apps, and Eventee is AI-empty in that race. With a 15-person team, the answer is not to match their whole AI roadmap. You would lose that fight and dilute the focus that makes Eventee good.

The opening is the attendee experience, where you already compete on craft. One sharp AI move beats ten shallow ones. The most valuable is probably an agenda and connections layer that genuinely understands an attendee's goals and quietly routes them to the right session and the right person, instead of dumping a directory on them. That is the part of an event that AI can actually improve, and it is squarely in your product's wheelhouse.

The skill is choosing that one bet and ignoring the rest of the AI checklist.

I run a practice that maps that for B2B SaaS at $500K to $5M ARR.
