Pedro,

Your own team names it plainly. The phishing landscape moves fast and the training content needs constant updates. That treadmill got worse this year, because attackers generate the lures now, in Portuguese and Spanish, at whatever quality they want.

Which means content volume stops being the scarce thing. A thousand training options in six languages was a moat when writing them was expensive. What stays scarce is the behavioural record you hold per organisation, across email and SMS, over years of campaigns.

KnowBe4 and Hoxhunt are pointing their AI at producing content. The side nobody has claimed is proving behaviour actually changed, which is the thing a security lead is buying in the first place.
