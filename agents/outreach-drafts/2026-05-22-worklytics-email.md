Philip,

You are already the dashboard for measuring AI adoption, and your WorkLab piece on agents and human agency tells me you see where this goes. So I will skip the part about AI mattering.

Two things I would press on. One, Microsoft's July admin update giving IT native Copilot adoption visibility is a real commoditization threat for that slice of your value, and you know it. Two, the more interesting opportunity sits on the other side of the same trend. Enterprises are shifting from AI tools to AI agents. The measurement layer that survives is the one agents themselves query to understand what is being adopted and what is working, not just the one humans read in Power BI. Being the data layer agents call, rather than a dashboard the Copilot admin center can imitate, is the moat.

That is a positioning and architecture decision, which suits a bootstrapped team that can move fast.

I run a practice that maps that for B2B SaaS at $500K to $5M ARR.
