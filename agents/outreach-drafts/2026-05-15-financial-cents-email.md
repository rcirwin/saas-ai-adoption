Subject: Karbon's AI vs. Financial Cents, the CTO question

Abdullah,

The April post titled "Bookkeeping Automation Examples That Actually Save Time" caught me. "Actually save time" is the right hedge. Most AI features in your category are not crossing that bar.

Karbon and Keeper and TaxDome are shipping AI fast. Financial Cents has a feature called Financial Cents AI but the depth is honest, not oversold. As CTO you sit on both questions at once: which AI bets ship into the product, and where AI moves the engineering team's own throughput.

I run a fixed-scope assessment for B2B SaaS at $500K to $5M ARR. The first deliverable is workflow mapping for your customers (CPAs, bookkeepers, the work they actually do). The fourth is the AI feature priority order. None of it is "ship more AI to look modern." Most of it is "here's the one that compounds, here's the four that don't."

Worth a 20-minute call to compare notes on what you're seeing with the workflow-template direction and where the next bet should go?
