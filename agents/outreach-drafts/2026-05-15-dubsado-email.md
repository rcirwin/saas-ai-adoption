Subject: Dubsado 3.0's Flow builder, the AI layer on top

Becca,

Dubsado 3.0 shipped in November is the right kind of platform rebuild. Node-based Flow builder is a substrate, not a feature. Most teams shipping that scale of redesign in 2025 bolted in AI to get the PR cycle. You shipped the rule-based foundation first. That sequence ages better.

The /llm-info page is the other tell. Most service-business SaaS founders aren't thinking about LLMs as a discovery channel yet. You are. Together those two moves make the next AI bet a more interesting decision than "ship a chatbot."

The natural next layer on a node-based Flow builder is AI-suggested flows, auto-drafted contracts from past patterns, smart routing inside the inbox. None of those have to ship at once. Most of HoneyBook's AI marketing is competing on the wrong axis (consumer-flavored AI features) while Dubsado is built for the operator who wants reliable automation.
