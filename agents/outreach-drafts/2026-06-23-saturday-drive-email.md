Subject: what changed when a form builder got the AI sequence right

Kevin,

The form builders getting AI right didn't lead with a form generator. They started one layer deeper, at what happens after submit: summarizing messy intake, routing it, turning a form into a small workflow. The generator is the demo. The post-submit intelligence is what gets used and keeps people from exporting to a spreadsheet.

Ninja Forms past 50M downloads means you sit on an enormous corpus of how real teams actually structure intake. That is the moat WPForms can't copy by shipping a generator. It's a question of which post-submit workflow to make the product own first.
