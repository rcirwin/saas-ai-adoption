Subject: Your AI Call for Interest, and ranking what came back

Kyle,

Your AI Call for Interest in February was the right instinct. You asked members what to build instead of guessing, which is the opposite of how most association tools bolted AI on.

The thing that survey tends to surface is a list of feature requests, not a sequence. Automated renewals and targeted comms both sound like one project, but for a small non-technical association staff they're very different bets in build cost and payoff. Getting the order wrong is where bootstrapped teams lose a quarter.

I run a fixed-scope assessment for B2B SaaS at $500K to $5M ARR, 8 to 12 weeks, four deliverables. It starts with workflow research on where your members and your own staff actually lose time, which is a useful read alongside what the Call for Interest told you.

Worth a 20-minute call to compare notes on how you're ranking what came back?
