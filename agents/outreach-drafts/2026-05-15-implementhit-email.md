Subject: Where AI lands in EHRs, behavior change is the bottleneck

Andres,

The 40% YoY growth at $1.4M ARR with 2,000+ hospitals is a strong line in a vertical that does not move fast. Behavior change as a PaaS, not a course catalog, is the right framing for healthcare.

The reason I am writing is what is happening upstream. Abridge, Suki, Nabla, and Epic's own AI rollouts are reshaping how physicians document and interact with EHRs in 2025-2026. Every one of those AI features creates a behavior change problem. Physicians have to be trained on what the AI is doing, when to trust it, when to override, and how to document the override. That is exactly the workflow your product is built around.

The audit I run with vertical SaaS founders in your category is not "add LLMs to your training content." It is the workflow question. Where does AI inside ImplementHIT specifically help physicians adopt other AI tools faster, and what changes about your behavior-change framework when the thing being adopted is itself adaptive.

Worth 20 minutes to compare notes? Happy to share patterns from adjacent vertical SaaS first.
