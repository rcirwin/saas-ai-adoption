Diony,

You and Dean already made the strategic call. Stepper as AI-native workflow automation, NL form generation, AI Insights. That puts you ahead of most form builders, so I am not here to suggest adding AI.

The next step is the interesting one. Stepper today is a workflow tool a human builds and runs. Typeform is pushing AI Agents in the same direction. The question that decides the next few years is whether Stepper becomes a surface that agents can drive, not just a builder humans configure. A form and a workflow that an external agent can fill, trigger, and act on turns Paperform from an app into infrastructure other automations rely on.

For a bootstrapped team at your scale, that is a positioning and design decision more than a ground-up rebuild, which is the good news.

I run a practice that maps the agent-readiness move for B2B SaaS at $500K to $5M ARR.

Worth 20 minutes to compare notes on Paperform specifically?
