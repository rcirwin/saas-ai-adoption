Jeroen,

You wrote the comparison of eight AI CRMs, so I will skip the part where I explain that AI features matter. You know that better than I do.

The thing I think is underexplored, and where Salesflare has a real structural edge, is one level up from features. As buyers start running sales work through agents, the question becomes which CRM an external agent can actually discover, read, and act inside. Most CRMs were built for a human clicking. Yours already auto-populates and structures the data without the human, which is exactly the substrate an agent needs.

That makes agent-readiness less of a rebuild for you and more of a positioning and surfacing decision. Attio is circling this. Being the CRM agents prefer to orchestrate is winnable.

I run a practice that maps the agent-readiness move for B2B SaaS at $500K to $5M ARR.
