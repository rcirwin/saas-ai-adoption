James,

You are on the ARF board shaping how the industry thinks about AI in advertising, and Swayable's causal-AI positioning is already deliberate. So I am not going to pretend there is an 'add AI' gap here. There is not.

The honest frontier for a platform like yours is on the other side. As marketing teams build agents to plan, generate, and decide on creative, the measurement layer that matters is the one those agents can query directly. A causal read on whether a concept will move opinion, available to an agent before it commits spend, is far more valuable than a report a human opens after the fact. The first creative-testing platform that is agent-queryable becomes the truth source agents check, rather than a tool humans visit.

That is a positioning and surface question, and it is squarely in the direction you are already pointed.

I run a practice that maps the agent-readiness move for B2B SaaS at $500K to $5M ARR.
