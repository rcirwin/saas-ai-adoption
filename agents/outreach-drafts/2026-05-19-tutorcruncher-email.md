Malachy,

Most tutoring SaaS is still deciding whether to do AI. You shipped a full lesson suite in May and spun up Bobbin AI as its own brand. That puts you in a different conversation entirely, so I will skip the "you should add AI" pitch.

Two questions are worth more at your stage. First, now that the suite is live, which features tutors actually adopt versus which looked good in a roadmap, because that is the real signal for what to build next. Second, as AI agents start acting on behalf of tutors and parents, can an agent discover and drive the TutorCruncher lesson workflow, or does it route around you? Being the AI mover in the category does not automatically make you agent-ready.

I run a practice that helps B2B SaaS teams figure out which AI investments are paying off and how to become agent-ready, grounded in user research. Mostly I would want to compare notes.

Worth a 20-minute call?
