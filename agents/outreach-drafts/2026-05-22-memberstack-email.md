Duncan,

You sit one layer above the build-a-site fight, on identity, payments, and access. That is a good place to be while Bolt, v0, and Lovable rip up the layer below you with AI. They make sites faster. They do not own who the member is or what they can do.

That is the interesting AI angle for Memberstack, and it is not 'add a chatbot.' As more of your customers' products, especially the AI-native founders you already serve, expose actions to agents, the auth and access layer becomes what decides whether an agent acting on a member's behalf is allowed to do something. Members as agent-routable identities, with permissions an agent can check. That turns Memberstack from a gate humans pass through into infrastructure agents have to respect.

You mentioned a Claude Code integration, so this is already on your radar.

I run a practice that maps the agent-readiness move for B2B SaaS at $500K to $5M ARR.

Worth 20 minutes to compare notes on Memberstack specifically?
