Twenty years in and The Customer Factor is still pointed squarely at window cleaners, pressure washers and carpet crews. Jobber and Housecall Pro went the other way, chasing every trade with venture money behind them. Staying narrow with a five person team was a choice.

It matters more now than it used to. Every field service tool is putting AI on scheduling and dispatch first, because routes demo well on a sales call.
