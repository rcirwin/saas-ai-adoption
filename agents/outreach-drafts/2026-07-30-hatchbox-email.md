Chris,

Hatchbox says plainly that support is people, no AI chatbots. That is a position, and it holds up better than most companies' AI pages do.

The part I keep turning over is that the same line does not have to govern the product. Vercel and Replit are shipping agents that deploy, and what they are still bad at is the part Hatchbox knows cold. Env vars, background workers, SSL, the failure that happens on someone else's server at 2am. A deploy tool that can read a failed deploy and explain it in plain terms is not a chatbot. It is the same support promise, moved earlier.

I run a fixed-scope assessment for B2B SaaS at $500K to $5M ARR, 8 to 12 weeks, four deliverables. It starts with workflow research, not feature ideation.
