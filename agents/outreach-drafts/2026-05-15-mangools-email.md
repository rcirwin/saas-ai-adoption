Subject: AI Search Watcher is the symptom, not the strategy

Peter,

Shipping AI Search Watcher this year was a sharper move than most SEO tools made. You named the structural shift (LLM intermediaries replacing Google for 30%+ of intent queries) and put a tracker on it. Ahrefs and Semrush are reacting late.

The reason I am writing is what AI Search Watcher implies but does not finish. Once LLM rank tracking is a feature, the underlying SEO workflow has to be redesigned around the fact that "ranking" no longer means one Google result page. It means cross-LLM cite-rate, ChatGPT memory-context inclusion, perplexity citation graph, AI Overview source set. The KWFinder primitive (keyword volume) starts to crack. Volume on a query that ChatGPT answers without citations is volume that does not convert.

The audit I run with bootstrapped SaaS founders shipping early AI features is the workflow question. What does Mangools as a product look like rebuilt around LLM intermediaries as the primary search surface, not Google plus a tracker bolted on. That is product strategy work, not feature ship work.

Worth 20 minutes to compare notes on what that looks like for Mangools specifically?
