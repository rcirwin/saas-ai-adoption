Hello,

Your blog series on HOA AI chatbots stood out, especially the post asking whether they are even legal. Most HOA software is racing to ship a chatbot without asking that. The caution is the right instinct.

It also points at a bigger question. A resident-facing chatbot answers FAQs. Useful, but it does not touch where HOA boards and managers actually lose time: violation tracking, payment chasing, running a clean vote, coordinating reservations. That is the manual admin load, and it churns over with every volunteer board turnover. AI that acts on that workflow is far more valuable than a bot that answers "when is trash day."

I run a practice that helps B2B SaaS teams figure out where AI genuinely fits the workflow and where it is just a bolt-on, grounded in real user research. You are clearly already thinking about this. I would mostly want to compare notes.

Worth a 20-minute call?
