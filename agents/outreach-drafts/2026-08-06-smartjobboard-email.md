Subject: AI Matching and the association renewal conversation

Rodion,

AI Matching shipping in 2024 put SmartJobBoard ahead of most of the association software market, so I will skip the part where I suggest adding AI.

The question I would sit with instead is who your buyer answers to. An association executive renews the job board because it justifies membership. A state agency renews because the process holds up when somebody asks how a candidate was ranked. Match quality serves both. Explaining the match serves only the second, and that is the one with procurement attached.

Peers will ship semantic search this year. Very few white-label vendors will be able to hand a public-sector client a defensible record of why a ranking came out the way it did, and 400 organizations of that shape is an unusual base to build it from.

I run a fixed-scope assessment for B2B SaaS at $500K to $5M ARR, 8 to 12 weeks, four deliverables. It starts with workflow research, not feature ideation.

Worth a 20-minute call to compare notes on where that research would start for SmartJobBoard?
