Filip,

BookingKoala has a real dispatch and automation engine, and it is rule-based. Rules are predictable, which is a genuine strength. They also break the moment reality gets messy: a cancellation cascade, an uneven crew day, a same-day surge.

That is exactly the gap an AI layer fills. Not a chatbot, but AI that makes the dispatch and rebooking decision when the rule tree runs out of branches. Jobber and Housecall Pro are moving in this direction, and in a category this competitive, the operator-facing intelligence is what will separate the booking engines.

I run a practice that helps B2B SaaS teams ($500K to $5M ARR) figure out where AI genuinely beats automation, which features to skip, and how to stay ahead of bigger competitors, based on real workflow research. Building this 100% founder-owned for eight years tells me you only add what earns its place.
