Subject: what changed when edtech catalogs got the AI sequence right

Jim,

The edtech tools pulling ahead on AI didn't win by generating worksheets on demand. MagicSchool and Eduaide made that a commodity inside a year. The ones that held ground started from the teacher's actual planning workflow and made their reviewed content the thing an assistant leans on, not competes with.

Twenty-six years of teacher-vetted resources is exactly the asset a generation-on-demand tool can't fake. The question I'd start from is whether Lesson Planet's catalog becomes the trusted layer a lesson-planning assistant calls, rather than a search box sitting next to a generator.
