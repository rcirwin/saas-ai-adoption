Ian,

Stripe Billing and Chargebee ship AI dunning and revenue-intelligence features on a quarterly drumbeat. For a mid-market billing platform, trying to match that arms race is a fight against companies with far deeper pockets, and it squeezes exactly the positioning you sit in.

So I would not chase it. The more interesting lane is the one they are not really running yet. Billing as something AI agents can operate. As more of a business's finance and ops work moves through agents, the systems that expose clean, agent-readable billing actions get orchestrated. The ones that assume a human in the dashboard get routed around.

Your Zapier-to-5000-apps DNA says you already think about being part of other people's workflows. Agent-readiness is the next version of that.

I run a practice that maps that move for B2B SaaS at $500K to $5M ARR.

Worth 20 minutes to compare notes on Billsby specifically?
