Subject: $1 per child, the roadmap underneath it

Subra,

A dollar per child per month is the whole strategy. It is what lets a 60-student Montessori school run the same admin stack as a district, and it is also what makes every feature decision a cost decision.

That is the part I would hold onto through the AI-powered launch. Most AI in school software is being priced and built for schools that have an IT director. Your buyer is a principal doing admin between classes, and what moves for her is not a better dashboard. It is that the parent email, the fee reminder and the field trip form get written and sent without anyone opening a spreadsheet.

Seventeen years of small-school workflow is a narrower and more useful base for that than anything Brightwheel is working from.

I run a fixed-scope assessment for B2B SaaS at $500K to $5M ARR, 8 to 12 weeks, four deliverables. It starts with workflow research, not feature ideation.

Worth a 20-minute call to compare notes on where that research would start for SchoolCues?
