Subject: HiBob's AI poaching and CharlieHR's silence

Rob,

HiBob's AI marketing has been dominant for the last 12 months and BambooHR is following hard. CharlieHR's quiet by comparison. From outside it reads two ways: either you've decided the AI-everywhere positioning is noise, or you're still working out which bets are real. Both are reasonable.

The category trap is shipping AI features because HiBob ships AI features. The opportunity for an SMB HRIS is the part of the HR workflow that AI actually shifts for a 25-person UK company. Onboarding, leave management, comp review, employee handbook lookup. Most of those have low-effort AI wins that compound. A few have AI bets that backfire (anything that touches a termination decision, for example).

I run a fixed-scope assessment for B2B SaaS at $500K to $5M ARR. The work maps workflow friction first, then sequences the AI bets. For CharlieHR the conversation is probably "where AI earns its place in the SMB HR day" rather than "match HiBob's roadmap."
