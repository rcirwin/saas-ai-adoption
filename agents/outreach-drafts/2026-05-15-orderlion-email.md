Subject: Choco, procurement agents, and Orderlion's other side

Stefan,

The Orderlion play (Vienna bootstrap to 500+ EU suppliers and 200+ ERP integrations) is well-executed vertical SaaS. AI Inbox converting email/WhatsApp/voicemail into ERP entries is a real workflow win on the order intake side.

The reason I am writing is the other side. Choco is positioning itself as the AI-native B2B food platform, and the more interesting bet they are making is the procurement-agent direction. Restaurant chefs and food-business operators will increasingly buy through AI agents that handle quotes, comparisons, and routine reordering. That changes the customer Orderlion is selling to. Suppliers will need to be discoverable to agents, not just to humans on a phone.

The audit I run with vertical SaaS founders in this position is the agent-readiness question. AI Inbox optimizes one side (parsing supplier input). The other side is whether Orderlion is structured so that procurement agents can discover, quote against, and orchestrate purchases through the platform. That is a different product strategy question than "more AI inside."

Worth 20 minutes to compare notes?
