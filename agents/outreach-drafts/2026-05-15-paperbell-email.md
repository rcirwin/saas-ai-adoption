Subject: How "no bots, no AI" maps to the team side

Laura,

Heard the Ep 814 episode on beating the venture-backed rival. The bootstrap-ops discipline you described, paired with the public "never bots or AI" promise on the customer side, is a clearer position than most coaching tools have.

That stance is also why I am not writing about adding AI to Paperbell. It would dilute exactly what you are selling.

The conversation I have been having with founders in that spot is a different one. Not customer-facing AI. Internal team time. Where does AI quietly remove 6 hours a week from your team's exception handling, support triage, payment dispute review, content QA. None of that touches the brand promise. None of it shows up in front of a coach.

Most teams skip that audit because they associate "AI" with "ship a chatbot." It is the opposite. For a 7-figure bootstrapped operation, the internal audit is the only AI conversation worth having.

Worth 20 minutes to compare notes on what that looks like inside a team like Paperbell's? Happy to share patterns from other bootstrapped operators first.
