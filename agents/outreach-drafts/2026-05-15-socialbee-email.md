Subject: SocialBee + Copilot as agent surface

Ovi,

Copilot is one of the most comprehensive AI suites in this category at your ARR. The interesting question isn't more AI features.

The pressure I keep watching in social SaaS is two-sided. Predis and Ocoya are coming from below on price with AI-native architecture. Hootsuite and Sprout have capital from above. SocialBee's flank in that fight is probably not "ship more AI features." It's whether Copilot stays a destination (marketers log in, generate, schedule) or becomes invokable by the marketer's broader agent stack. A brand strategy agent generates the calendar in SocialBee automatically. A campaign agent triggers cross-channel posts without anyone opening the dashboard.

That's a different moat than caption-generation quality. It's about being the orchestratable layer in a marketing-agent workflow.

The bootstrapped founders I've been comparing notes with at your ARR keep landing on this exact framing.
