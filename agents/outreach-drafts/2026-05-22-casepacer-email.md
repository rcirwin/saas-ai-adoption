Tony,

CP Assist is on your site as 'Soon,' which means you are mid-decision on the most important product call you will make this year. What should a legal AI assistant for a personal-injury firm actually do.

That is exactly the moment to get right, because Filevine is pushing AI into mass tort with VC money and Clio shipped Duo. Copying their generic 'ask the assistant a question' approach would waste your niche. The advantage you have is depth in PI and mass-tort workflow. CP Assist should be built around where those firms actually lose hours. Medical-record summarization, demand-letter drafting, intake qualification, deadline triage across thousands of plaintiffs. Not a chatbot bolted on the side.

Defining what to build before you build it is the cheapest, highest-impact move you will ever make, and it is the part most teams skip.

I run a practice that maps exactly that for vertical SaaS at $500K to $5M ARR.

Worth 20 minutes to compare notes on CP Assist specifically?
