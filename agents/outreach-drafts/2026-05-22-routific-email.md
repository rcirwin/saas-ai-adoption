Marc,

With an operations-research background and 179 ML models behind the ETAs, you do not need anyone telling you to add AI. The AI is already the product. So I will skip that.

Where I think the next moat sits is how that engine gets driven. Today a dispatcher operates it through a UI. As logistics teams adopt agents, the routing engine that an external agent or a natural-language request can orchestrate becomes the one that gets embedded into everyone's workflow, while UI-only tools get treated as a destination people have to remember to visit. Your engine is genuinely good enough to be the thing other systems call. The question is whether it is structured to be called.

Circuit is outspending you on growth. Being the routing brain agents prefer to orchestrate is a lane spending cannot simply buy.

I run a practice that maps the agent-readiness move for B2B SaaS at $500K to $5M ARR.
