Akhilesh,

You have already shipped Smart Agent, AI Copilot, and IT Autopilot. So I am not writing to suggest you add AI. You are ahead of most of your category there.

Two things are worth a sharper look when you are bootstrapped against players who raised $100M-plus. First, validation. With AI features shipped fast, it is easy to have a feature list that does not perfectly match where reps actually lose time. A short workflow audit tells you which of those AI features earn their keep and which are dragging maintenance. Second, agent-readiness. As buyers run outreach through their own agents, the question is whether SmartReach is something an external agent can drive, not just a UI a human logs into.

That second one is where a lean, sharp team can out-position the funded incumbents.

I run a practice that maps both for B2B SaaS at $500K to $5M ARR.

Worth 20 minutes to compare notes on SmartReach specifically?
