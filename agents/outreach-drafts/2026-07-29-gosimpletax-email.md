Mike,

Thirty years inside HMRC turned into a rules engine is not something Earnr or a new tax app assembles quickly, and I think that is underrated in how the UK tax category sorts itself out.

The reason I would not rush that engine toward a model is that tax is one of the few categories where a plausible wrong answer is worse than no answer. Somebody carries that number. What does look ready is everything around the return: receipts, chasing missing records, the quarterly rhythm landlords are about to feel under MTD. That is where a wrong guess is recoverable and where the hours actually go.

I run a fixed-scope assessment for B2B SaaS at $500K to $5M ARR, 8 to 12 weeks, four deliverables. It starts with workflow research, not feature ideation.

Worth a 20-minute call to compare notes on where that research would start for GoSimpleTax?
