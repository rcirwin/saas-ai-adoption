Subject: the OKR check-in is a retyped number

Sten,

Every OKR tool shipped the same three AI features inside about a year. Draft the objective from a prompt, summarize the check-in, flag the goal that is slipping. AI Mode does all three, and does them cleanly.

All three are text about work. None of them touch the actual failure mode, which is that a check-in is a person retyping a number that already exists in a system of record somewhere else.

You watched Jira become the thing everything else reads from. That is the fork I would want to argue about. Is Tability trying to be the system of record for goals, or the layer that reads from the other systems and stops asking humans to retype? The AI answer is completely different depending on which one it is.

Happy to share the directional read on where I would start, no commitment. Open to a short call or just trading notes here.

Founder, Future Ready Studio
