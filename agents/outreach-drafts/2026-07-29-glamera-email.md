Mohamed,

Half a billion riyals of transactions across Egypt and Saudi is a lot of behavioural history for a company founded in 2019, and it is a different asset from the booking software itself.

The decision I would look at first is chair utilization. Salon owners lose margin to no-shows and idle stylists, and that is a forecasting problem sitting on data you already hold: who books what, at which branch, at what hour, and who does not turn up. Fresha and Booksy are adding AI to the booking surface. Predicting demand per branch and per stylist is the harder square, and running two markets with different working weeks and holiday calendars makes it harder still for anyone outside the region to copy.

I run a fixed-scope assessment for B2B SaaS at $500K to $5M ARR, 8 to 12 weeks, four deliverables. It starts with workflow research, not feature ideation.

Worth a 20-minute call to compare notes on where that research would start for Glamera?
