Tyler,

I have heard you push back on AI hype on Startup to Last, and I mostly agree with you. Most SMB CRM AI is feature theater stacked on top of products that were already too complicated.

So this is not a pitch to add AI. It is the opposite. The interesting work for a product whose entire promise is 'less annoying' is figuring out the one or two places AI could quietly remove a chore without adding a single thing the user has to learn. Drafting the follow-up they were going to write anyway. Summarizing a long contact history into the next action. Everything else, deliberately skip.

The discipline of saying no to most AI is exactly what your brand is built on. Doing it on purpose beats reacting to HubSpot's roadmap.

That decision, which AI earns its place and which to ignore, is what I map for B2B SaaS at $500K to $5M ARR.

Worth 20 minutes to compare notes? I suspect we would agree on most of it.
