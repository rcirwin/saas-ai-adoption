Subject: what changed when email platforms got the sequence right

Vinoop,

The pattern I keep seeing in email marketing: generation goes first and commoditizes fastest. Subject lines and body copy are table stakes at every ESP now, so a year of that work buys parity, not distance. The platforms that got the sequence right pointed the model at the send decision instead, timing, segment and suppression, because that is where their own delivery telemetry is an input nobody else holds.

Mailercloud moves around 6 billion messages a month and holds 98% client retention. That telemetry is the asset the writing assistant is not using. AMP makes it more interesting again, because an interactive message is one of the few email surfaces an agent could act inside rather than just read.
