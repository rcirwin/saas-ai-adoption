JuraLaw has the kind of asset most legal-AI startups would love and cannot build. 2,600-plus jurisdictions of court-deadline rules, maintained with Law Bulletin Media's reputation behind it since 1854. That authority is the moat.

The risk is that legal AI adoption roughly doubled in the past year, and a deadline tool that stays purely rule-based starts to look like the last manual step in an otherwise AI-assisted firm. The opportunity is the opposite of chasing the Harvey-style hype. It is using AI to make your deadline intelligence proactive. Reading a new filing or order and telling a firm which deadlines just changed across which jurisdictions, before a paralegal has to look it up. AI built on the rules data you already own and trust.

For a 170-year institution, the move is using AI to extend the authority, not to imitate startups.
