Subject: AI inside the CRM is shipped, what about AI orchestrating it

Jeroen,

The 8-AI-CRMs comparison post and the "Intelligent CRM" positioning is one of the clearer AI strategies in SMB CRM. Timeline summaries, next-step suggestions, AI Q&A on contacts and companies are shipped, working, and used. At 7 people that is real product velocity.

The reason I am writing is what is one leg past that. Attio is building toward agent-orchestratable CRM, where external AI agents (Cursor, Claude, custom sales agents) read, write, and act on CRM data as the primary interface for ops users. That is a different design surface than "AI features inside the UI." HubSpot Breeze and Pipedrive's AI assistant are still racing inside the UI. The agent-orchestratable layer is the next moat and nobody has built it well yet in SMB.

The audit I run with founders who are this far ahead on AI inside the product is the orchestration question. What does Salesflare look like designed so a sales agent in Cursor (or Cal AI, or a custom workflow agent) treats it as a system of record, not a destination. That is product strategy work, not feature ship work.

Worth 20 minutes to compare notes?
