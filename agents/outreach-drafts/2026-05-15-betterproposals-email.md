Subject: AI With Common Sense, the operations layer

Adam,

The 12,000-companies-actively-screwing-up-deals quote is one of the best AI takes I've read this year. "AI with common sense" is the construction the rest of the category needs and won't borrow because it would slow their roadmaps down.

Reading both posts back to back, the position is clear. Proposal AI ships where it helps the customer not screw up. AI-written content does not ship because giving 12,000 service businesses a way to produce worse proposals at scale is the opposite of the job. Same operator instinct shows up in the "human-only company" line.

I run a fixed-scope assessment for B2B SaaS at $500K to $5M ARR. The work isn't "ship more AI." It's mapping where AI moves the operator's day at a 9-person bootstrapped team. Internal ops, sales workflow, support triage, content production. The same principled-restraint applied to the team, not just the product.

Worth 20 minutes to compare notes on where common-sense AI does and doesn't earn its place?
