Subject: Karbon's AI Coworker and the Jetpack play

David,

Growing Your Firm has been the most consistent operator-quality podcast in the CPA software space for years. The Jetpack template library, cascading deadlines, and team progress tracking is exactly the workflow primitive accounting firms actually use day to day.

The reason I am writing is Karbon's AI Coworker positioning and what Financial Cents shipped this year. Karbon is reframing the category from "workflow management" to "AI handles routine work, humans handle exceptions." That is a category narrative shift, not a feature ship. Once boards and managing partners absorb that framing, "workflow management without AI" becomes harder to defend on a sales call.

The audit I run with vertical SaaS founders in this position is not "match Karbon feature for feature." It is the workflow-redesign question. Where does AI inside Jetpack actually save a CPA firm 8 hours a week without bolting on another chatbot. Tax-prep checklist exception handling. Recurring-engagement onboarding. Cascading-deadline triage when capacity shifts.

Worth 20 minutes to compare notes? Happy to share what I am seeing in adjacent CPA software first.
