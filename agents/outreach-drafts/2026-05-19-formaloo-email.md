Farokh,

I saw the 4.0 keynote framed as rebuilding Formaloo around the AI era. That's a bigger claim than most no-code teams make, and I respect it.

I work with B2B SaaS founders on AI strategy, and the pattern I watch for is whether an AI rebuild moved a real number or mostly added to the feature list. With breadth like yours, forms plus CRM plus portals plus apps, the risk is the AI generator and agent bots each impress in a demo but don't change the workflow a customer actually lives in.

Not pitching anything. I'm genuinely curious whether 4.0 moved retention or activation versus new-account appeal. Happy to compare notes on what I'm seeing across no-code if it's useful.
