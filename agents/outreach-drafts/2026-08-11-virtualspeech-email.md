Dom,

The bet in VirtualSpeech was always the room, not the feedback. Yoodli can tell someone their pace was fast. It does not put them in front of an audience that reacts badly.

Which is why the AI-Powered framing on the site is the part I would push on. Conversational models are getting good at being the counterpart, and that closes the distance between a coaching tool and a simulation faster than the headset ever did. The scarce asset stops being the VR and becomes scenario design. How hard the simulated person pushes back, tuned for a Vodafone rollout versus an Accenture one, is not a model capability. It is a research output.

That is a workflow question before it is a model question, and it is where I would start.
