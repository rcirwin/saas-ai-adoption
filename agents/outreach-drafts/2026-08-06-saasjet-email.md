Subject: Time in Status, the roadmap underneath it

Anatoliy,

Time in Status looks like a reporting app. Underneath, it is a record of how long work actually sat in each state, accumulated across 1500 companies including Forbes and Grammarly.

That distinction matters more this year than last. Rovo will answer "what is the status of this epic" natively and for free, because that question reads current state. It will not answer "is this team's cycle time degrading, and since which sprint", because that needs history somebody chose to keep. Native tooling tends to take the first question and leave the second one alone.

Sixty people is real build capacity to point at that gap, and the Atlassian plus Monday plus Google footprint means you get to test the answer in more than one market.

I run a fixed-scope assessment for B2B SaaS at $500K to $5M ARR, 8 to 12 weeks, four deliverables. It starts with workflow research, not feature ideation.

Worth a 20-minute call to compare notes on where that research would start for SaaSJet?
