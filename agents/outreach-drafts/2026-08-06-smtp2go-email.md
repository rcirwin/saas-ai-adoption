Subject: what changed when email infrastructure got the sequence right

Charlie,

An observation about email infrastructure that I think holds up. The vendors adding AI are adding it to content, subject-line scoring and send-time suggestions. That is the layer that commoditizes first, because every provider gets the same models. Delivering into a regional ISP in one of 130 countries, and knowing why last Tuesday's batch soft-bounced there, is not a model problem. It is twenty years of accumulated behaviour that a new entrant cannot buy.

That gap widens rather than narrows as software starts sending on its own. When a workflow triggers the mail instead of a marketer, nobody is watching a dashboard afterwards, so the endpoint has to be right the first time and has to say clearly when it was not.

If useful, I can share the directional read on where I'd start with SMTP2GO, no commitment. Open to a short call or just trading notes here.
