Adam,

You have written openly about ChatGPT changing how projections get built. Most founders in your spot stay quiet about that. Naming it is the first hard step, so I will skip straight to the second.

If a model can draft a projection, then the spreadsheet template is no longer the moat. What survives is the part ChatGPT cannot do well: CPA judgment, the lender's actual expectations, the credibility a bank needs to see, the workflow of getting an SBA application approved rather than just drafted. The question is whether ProjectionHub leans into that and rebuilds the product around it, and whether AI inside your tool can make the CPA layer faster instead of competing with it.

I run a practice that helps B2B SaaS teams work out which AI moves protect the business and which accelerate the commoditization, all grounded in real user research.

Worth a 20-minute call to think it through?
