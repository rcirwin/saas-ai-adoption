Subject: the photographer workflow chain, and who orchestrates it

Gurpreet,

The shift I am watching in vertical-creative SaaS: the workflow chain (booking → shoot → cull → gallery → delivery → invoice) is becoming the surface that creative-AI assistants will try to orchestrate end to end. ShootProof, Pic-Time, and Pixpa all sit on different pieces of that chain today.

The bet that compounds is owning more of the chain in a way the AI assistant has to call into. AI Text Writer with Brand Profile context is interesting because Brand Profile is a piece of context the orchestrating AI does not have, so it has to come back to Pixpa. That is the right shape. The question is which other pieces of the chain can get the same treatment, and which ones get routed around to a horizontal AI.

Worth a 20-minute call to compare notes on where I would start that research for Pixpa?
