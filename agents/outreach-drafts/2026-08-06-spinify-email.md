Subject: what changed when sales gamification got the sequence right

Matt,

A category observation before anything else. Gamification works because a rep changes behaviour when the behaviour is counted. That premise assumes the rep is the one doing the counted thing. Once an assistant drafts the sequence and logs the activity, activity counts inflate and stop separating anyone, which quietly breaks a leaderboard rather than improving it.

The Coaching Agent tells me you are well past the question of whether AI belongs in the product. The harder one is what stays worth counting. Discovery calls that changed a deal's direction, saves after a bad demo, the judgement moments an assistant cannot fake, and whether the CRM data you already pull is enough to identify them.
