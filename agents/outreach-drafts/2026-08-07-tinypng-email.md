Michiel,

TinyPNG has an unusual shape. A free web tool people visit by name, and a paid developer API sitting behind the same compression work. Twelve years and a six person team, with the free half doing the distribution for the paid half.

The distribution half is the exposed one. Format decisions are moving into build pipelines and CDNs that pick WebP or AVIF without anyone opening a browser tab, and image volume keeps climbing because models generate them now. Compression stops being a destination and becomes a call something else makes on the way past.

Fine if Tinify is the thing being called. Harder if the habit of typing tinypng.com was quietly doing more work than the API ever did.

I run a fixed-scope assessment for B2B SaaS at $500K to $5M ARR, 8 to 12 weeks, four deliverables. It starts with workflow research, not feature ideation.

Worth 20 minutes comparing notes on where that research would start?
