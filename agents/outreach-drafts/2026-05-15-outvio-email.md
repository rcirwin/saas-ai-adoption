Subject: Four pillars and four AI-native competitors

Juan,

The Outvio bet (4 pillars consolidated for D2C and 3PLs across Europe) is the right contrarian play in a category where most players pick one pillar and go deep. 100+ couriers and 30+ CMS integrations is real infrastructure work.

The reason I am writing is the cost structure of that bet. Each pillar is being attacked by a different AI-native competitor. Gorgias and Decagon are AI-native on helpdesk. Loop and Returnly on returns. ShipBob on shipping operations. Outvio Desk's AI Recognition is reasonable, but it solves one pillar while the other three still need parity. The consolidation bet only works if each pillar is at least as good as the AI-native single-pillar player.

The audit I run with multi-pillar ops platforms is the prioritization question. Which pillar gets real AI investment first, where workflow research reveals the friction is actually highest, and what stays "good enough" for now. That is a workflow audit, not a feature roadmap exercise.

Worth 20 minutes to compare notes on what that looks like across the 4 pillars?
