Foti,

The pattern I keep seeing in curated marketplaces is that the first AI goes to the demand side. Matching, search, discovery, because it demos well and the data is already sitting there.

The compounding work is on the supply side. Which mentors get rebooked and why, and what a vetting process should catch before someone is listed at all. GrowthMentor's whole claim is curation, and holding that standard is manual work that grows with the roster. As general growth advice gets cheap, the vetting and the rebooking evidence are closer to what a member is actually paying for than the match itself.
