Your public stance on AI at Cliniko is the clearest founder signal I have read in this space. Principles first, no shipping AI for the sake of saying you shipped AI.

I am not writing to talk you out of that. I run a practice built on the same idea. Start with the problem, not the technology. Most AI in healthcare software solves what is easy to demo, not what drains a clinic.
