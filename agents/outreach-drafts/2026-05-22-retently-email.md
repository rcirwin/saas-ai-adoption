You already shipped the hard part. An AI CX agent that reads surveys, reviews, and tickets, then says what is breaking and what to do, is ahead of where most feedback tools are.

The interesting next move for a nine-person team is not building more AI. It is connection. Your CX agent has the freshest read on customer sentiment in the company, but that signal is trapped inside Retently. As companies stand up their own internal agents for support, product, and ops, the question becomes whether those agents can query yours. The first CX platform that is agent-readable, not just AI-powered, becomes the sentiment layer the whole org's automation relies on.

That is a positioning and surface decision more than a build, which is exactly what suits a lean team.
