Subject: Tarifica's synthesis layer, and the consulting ceiling

Richard,

Bootstrapping Tarifica since 2008 with no VC, across 140-plus countries, is a rare position. The dataset is the moat.

The structural tension I would name: collecting and validating pricing data is labor-heavy, and insight synthesis is consulting-heavy. Both scale with headcount, not with software. PlanDetector shows you already think in real-time monitoring terms. The next layer is AI that does the synthesis work an analyst does today, so a 26-person team punches even further above its weight.

I run a practice that helps B2B SaaS teams figure out which AI opportunities matter and which to skip, grounded in real workflow research. For a data business, the data-readiness assessment is usually where the surprises are.
