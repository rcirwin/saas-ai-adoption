Subject: Plumbing-first membership vs. community-AI race

Ward,

The MemberSpace identity (membership plumbing on top of any site builder) is a different posture than Circle or Skool. They are building communities. You are making any WordPress or Squarespace site into a membership business. That distinction is the moat.

The reason I am writing is what is happening on the adjacent side. Circle and Skool are pushing AI for community engagement. Kajabi is going AI-native creator platform. Outseta is your bootstrapped peer and has gone AI-explicit. The community-AI race is loud and not really yours to run.

The interesting audit for MemberSpace is the workflow that is yours. Retention is the membership business in one word. Content recommendations across gated tiers. Churn signals from engagement drop. Onboarding flows that adapt to creator type. That is AI inside the membership plumbing, not AI inside community engagement. It deepens the moat instead of muddying the positioning.

That is the audit I run with bootstrapped founders in adjacent categories. Worth 20 minutes to compare notes on what that looks like for MemberSpace?
