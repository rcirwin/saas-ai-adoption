Subject: the capture layer, and what sits on top of it

Arne,

Most of the time-tracking category is arguing about the model. Memtime's real difference sits upstream of that. Capturing programs, documents, calendars and mail passively, accurately enough that someone can reconstruct a day, is the hard engineering. Categorizing what was captured is now the easy half.

That inversion is worth sitting with. Timely carries the AI-native label, but the label is on the part that got cheap. Capture is what any model needs to be useful at all, and rebuilding it takes the years you already spent since timeBro.

The open question I would work is which decision the categorized data should drive. Billing accuracy, staffing, or scope creep on a project. Those are three different products.

I run a fixed-scope assessment for B2B SaaS at $500K to $5M ARR, 8 to 12 weeks, four deliverables. It starts with workflow research, not feature ideation.

Worth a 20-minute call to compare notes on where that research would start for Memtime?
