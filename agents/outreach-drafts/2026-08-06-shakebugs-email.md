Subject: 50 data points per report, the roadmap underneath it

Vladimir,

Shake's bet has always been capture. Fifty-plus data points attached automatically to every report, so a developer never has to interview the tester about what they were doing.

That bet reads differently now that Instabug has shipped AI triage and Sentry is bolting it on. Triage and summarization converge quickly, because every vendor gets the same models within a year of each other. What does not converge is what was in the payload at the moment the bug happened. A model that can see the HTTP log, the app version and the reproduction steps writes a materially different answer than one reading a free-text complaint.

Ford and Qantas did not pick Shake for a summary. They picked it because the report arrives complete.

I run a fixed-scope assessment for B2B SaaS at $500K to $5M ARR, 8 to 12 weeks, four deliverables. It starts with workflow research, not feature ideation.

Worth a 20-minute call to compare notes on where that research would start for Shake?
