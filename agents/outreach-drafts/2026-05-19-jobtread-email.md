Subject: AI Connector, 12 months in

Eric,

Saw JobTread shipped the AI Connector with native Claude and ChatGPT. Smart move to get the surface live early.

I spend my time with B2B SaaS founders on exactly this question, and the pattern I keep seeing is that the connector is the easy part. The hard part is which contractor workflows actually get delegated once the novelty wears off. Estimating? Daily logs? Change orders? Construction adopts slowly, so the first workflow that truly sticks tends to define the roadmap.

Not pitching anything here. I'm genuinely curious, 12 months from launch, which workflows you expect contractors to hand over versus keep manual. Happy to share the patterns I'm seeing across other workflow-heavy SaaS if useful.
