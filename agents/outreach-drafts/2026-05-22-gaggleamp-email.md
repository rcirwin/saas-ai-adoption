Glenn,

Fifteen years in employee advocacy means you know the real problem is not the content. It is participation. Most employees do not engage with the program, and AI-generated copy variations, the feature you and DSMN8 both shipped, do not move that number. They make the posts that do happen slightly easier to write.

That is why the AI copy race is the wrong race. The valuable AI move points at adoption. Knowing which employee will actually share, what topic will land for them, the right moment to nudge, and removing the friction that makes them ignore the program in the first place. AI aimed at behavior, not at paraphrasing.

Solve participation and the whole product gets more valuable. Match DSMN8 on copy variations and you have spent effort on the part that was never the bottleneck.

I run a practice that maps where AI fits the actual workflow problem for B2B SaaS at $500K to $5M ARR.

Worth 20 minutes to compare notes on GaggleAMP specifically?
