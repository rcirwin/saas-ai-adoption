Romain,

AI captions, AI hashtags, AI posting times. You shipped them, and so did Buffer, Later, and everyone else. That layer is now commoditized, which is fine, because it was never your differentiator anyway.

Your differentiator is the word in your own positioning. Analytics-first. That is exactly where AI gets interesting and where most social tools are weak. Not 'write me a caption' but 'tell me which of my 200 posts this quarter actually moved the metric, why, and what to do more of next week.' AI that turns analytics into a decision, across seven networks, is hard to copy and squarely in your DNA.

Sprout and Hootsuite can outspend you on generic AI. They cannot easily out-analyze you.

I run a practice that maps which AI moves fit a product's real strength for B2B SaaS at $500K to $5M ARR.

Worth 20 minutes to compare notes on Iconosquare specifically?
