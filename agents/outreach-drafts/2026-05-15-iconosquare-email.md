Subject: Analytics-first DNA in an AI-caption race

Romain,

Iconosquare's analytics-first positioning is the most defensible thing about the product. Buffer and Later are scheduling-first. Sprout and Hootsuite are enterprise-suite-first. You are the analytics depth play for 10K+ brands across 7 networks. That is real differentiation.

The captions, hashtags, and posting-time AI is reasonable, but it is the same feature-list every social tool is shipping. FeedHive and Predis.ai are AI-native and have a 12-month head start on bolt-on parity. Sprout has the funding to out-ship on captions.

The interesting question is not whether you need more caption AI. It is whether the analytics DNA can be rebuilt as the workflow AI nobody else has. Multi-network campaign orchestration that learns from cross-network performance. Report generation that takes 4 hours and becomes a 4-minute review. Insight surfacing that beats "here are your numbers" with "here is what to do."

That is the audit I run with bootstrapped founders in workflow-heavy categories. Worth 20 minutes to compare notes on Iconosquare specifically?
