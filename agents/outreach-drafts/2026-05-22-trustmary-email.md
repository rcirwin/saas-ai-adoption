'Get found in AI search' tells me your instinct is right. Reviews are increasingly read and cited by ChatGPT and Gemini, not just shown on a website. The question is whether that stays a single feature or becomes the spine of the product.

The strategic version is bigger. If AI assistants are the new place buyers ask for recommendations, then the entire review workflow, what you collect, how you structure it, what you surface, should be designed for that. Which testimonials an AI will actually cite, and why. That is a different product decision than a badge that says AI-search-ready.

For a bootstrapped team near the start of the AI-search shift, getting that decision right early is a real advantage over Birdeye and Trustpilot, who are slower to turn.
