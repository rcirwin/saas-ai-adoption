Subject: Folderly's 3 stages and one AI loop

Vladislav,

The Belkins-to-Folderly arc and the Inbox Insights / Folderly / Pulse pipeline is the right shape for a deliverability product. Real workflow, not a tool.

The reason I am writing is what is happening above the pipeline. EmailGen is a sensible bolt-on for sequence generation. But Instantly and the AI-native cold email platforms are not adding deliverability features. They are absorbing deliverability into a single orchestration loop where the AI decides send timing, recipient routing, and remediation in one pass. That collapses three of your stages into one agent decision.

I am not writing about adding more AI to EmailGen. The interesting audit is the other way around. Where does the 3-stage deliverability workflow get rebuilt as an orchestration agent so Folderly is the one absorbing the category, not being absorbed.

That is the conversation I have been having with bootstrapped founders in the cold email and deliverability space. Worth 20 minutes to compare notes?
