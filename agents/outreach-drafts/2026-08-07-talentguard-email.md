Linda,

You put WorkforceGPT in market in 2023 and rebuilt the positioning around AI talent intelligence. Early for this category, and it means the should-we-add-AI conversation is closed on your side. That is not the one worth having.

Here is the one I would have. Skills inference is converging. Eightfold, Gloat and Beamery raise capital specifically to make a model read an HRIS and guess at what someone can do, and that guess gets cheaper every quarter. Capital buys inference.

What capital does not buy is fifteen years of competency frameworks that HR leaders actually signed off on, plus the record of which inferred skill preceded a real internal move. Inference on its own is a confident list. Inference against that record is evidence a CHRO can defend in a budget meeting.

I run a fixed-scope assessment for B2B SaaS at $500K to $5M ARR, 8 to 12 weeks, four deliverables. It starts with workflow research, not feature ideation.

Worth 20 minutes on where that research would start for TalentGuard?
