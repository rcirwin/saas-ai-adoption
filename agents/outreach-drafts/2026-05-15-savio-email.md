Subject: Savio's judgment layer, the AI question underneath

Ryan,

Revenue-weighted prioritization is one of the few things AI summarization tools cannot do well. They can group similar requests. They cannot decide which group matters more to the business this quarter. That call is what your product is built around, and it's the part of feedback work AI keeps stumbling on.

The recent Canny and UserVoice alternative roundups list BuildBetter and Bagel as the AI-native options and skip Savio. That's a positioning question, not a product question. Most of those tools are synthesizing transcripts. You're routing decisions.

I run a fixed-scope assessment for B2B SaaS at $500K to $5M ARR. The work is mapping where AI actually saves your team and your customers time, and where AI is noise that gets in the way of the judgment work. For a feedback platform, the synthesis layer is the obvious bet, but the more interesting one is usually the customer-success workflow on top of it.

Worth a 20-minute call?
