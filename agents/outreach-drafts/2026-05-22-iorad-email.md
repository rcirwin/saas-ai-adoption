Sundeep,

Scribe Capture auto-generates tutorials now. Supademo and Tango have AI modes. The capture-first positioning that built iorad over a decade is the exact thing this wave is commoditizing, and I suspect you feel that.

But auto-generation is also a trap for your competitors. Anyone can spit out a walkthrough. Almost no one can prove whether learners actually adopted the workflow afterward. That is your asset. 30 million learners and ten years of engagement data is something Scribe cannot manufacture.

So the move isn't to win the generation race. It is to make iorad the layer that knows which tutorials drive real adoption and adapts them automatically when they stop working. AI pointed at outcomes, not just authoring.

I run a practice that maps which AI moves fit a product's real moat for B2B SaaS at $500K to $5M ARR.

Worth 20 minutes to compare notes on iorad specifically?
