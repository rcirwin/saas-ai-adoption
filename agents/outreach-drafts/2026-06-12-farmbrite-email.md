Subject: what changes when small-farm tools fix data entry, not dashboards

Ian,

The thing I keep seeing in farm software: the compliance and USDA record-keeping burden is not a reporting problem, it is a capture problem. The data never gets in cleanly because it happens in the field, not at a desk.

That is where AI actually helps small operators: photo-to-livestock-event, voice notes from the barn, USDA-ready summaries from records you already keep. It respects the budget constraint instead of adding a dashboard nobody has time to open.

You and Janine built Farmbrite for your own farm with no outside investors, so you have always had to ask whether a feature earns its keep. That is exactly the lens this question wants.
