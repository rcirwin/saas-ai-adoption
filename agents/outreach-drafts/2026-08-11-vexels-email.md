Subject: 43,000 vectors, the roadmap underneath it

Matias,

The library was the asset when drawing the t-shirt graphic was the expensive part. Ten years of vectors, mockups and merch templates was a real head start, and it funded the company without outside money.

Generation moved that, though not for the reason people usually give. Recraft does not draw better than your artists. The scarce input just changed. What a POD seller needs now is not an image. It is knowing which niche is about to sell, and a licence that holds up when a marketplace asks who owns the art. Both of those sit on your side of the business and neither is in a model.

So the question I would want to answer first is not which AI feature ships next. It is which of those two assets compounds once generation is free.

I run a fixed-scope assessment for B2B SaaS at $500K to $5M ARR, 8 to 12 weeks, four deliverables. It starts with workflow research, not feature ideation.

Worth a 20-minute call to compare notes on where that research would start for Vexels?
