Krunal,

Review Nuggets is the right instinct and it is ahead of most apps at your price point. Summarizing free text so a shopper does not read forty entries is real work, and Judge.me made you earn it.

The part I would push on is where that summary gets read. Reviews are the one category being squeezed from both ends. Models write them, and now models read them. A shopper's assistant increasingly consumes your widget's content and hands over a verdict before anyone scrolls to it.
