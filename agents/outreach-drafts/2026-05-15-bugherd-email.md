Subject: BugHerd AI in beta, the second bet

Alan,

"The startup that refuses to die" is a great founder essay. The throughline (incremental survival, careful bets, no hype) shows up in how BugHerd has shipped AI too. BugHerd AI in beta is the right cadence. Hotjar's funding lets them race; you don't have to.

The harder question is the one after the beta. First AI feature is usually a bolt-on. Second is the one that decides whether AI sits inside the workflow or stays as a sidebar. For visual bug tracking with agency-side QA workflows, the candidates are very different from what Hotjar is shipping. Bug triage routing, auto-categorization across client projects, agent-readiness for QA tools that will start using BugHerd via MCP.

I run a fixed-scope assessment for B2B SaaS at $500K to $5M ARR. The work maps workflow friction first, then sequences the AI bets. For BugHerd the framing is "what does the second AI bet need to do that the first didn't."

Worth 20 minutes to compare notes on what you're seeing in the beta and where it points next?
