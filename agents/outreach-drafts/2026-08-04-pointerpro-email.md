Stefan,

The pattern across assessment and quiz platforms is that generated report text converges fast. Outgrow, Typeform and anyone with an API key can write a personalised paragraph back to a respondent, and within a year or two nobody wins on that.

What does not converge is the scoring model underneath. Your consultants bring their own methodology and their own read of what a given answer means, and Pointerpro is where that logic lives. The prompt widget in the report builder rides on top of it.

So the interesting question is not more generation. It is whether the benchmark data across all those respondents becomes a product in its own right rather than a byproduct of running assessments.
