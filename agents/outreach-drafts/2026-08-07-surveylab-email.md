Piotr,

Prompt to questionnaire generation is table stakes now. SurveyMonkey, Typeform, Jotform and Qualtrics all ship a version of it, and so do you. The category settled that half in about eighteen months.

What has not settled is knowing who answered. Respondents are starting to hand forms to assistants, and once that is normal, seven million surveys of response data quietly changes meaning. Verified human response stops being an assumption and becomes a claim you have to make on purpose.

You already sell on EU privacy and data handling, so the machinery for making defensible claims about data is something you have built once. Provenance sits right next to it.

If useful, I can share the directional read on where I would start with SurveyLab, no commitment. Open to a short call or just trading notes here.
