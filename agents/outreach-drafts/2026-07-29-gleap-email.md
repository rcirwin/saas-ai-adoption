Lukas,

In bug reporting the AI everyone is racing toward is the same AI. Summarize the report, guess the severity, draft the reply. Jam, Marker and the Linear plus Sentry crowd are all converging there, and a model with a decent prompt closes that gap in a quarter.

What does not converge is capture. A summary is only as good as the replay, console state and environment sitting behind it, and getting that reliably out of a real user's browser is engineering rather than prompting. Gleap already did the expensive half, so Kai sits on top of something most of the category cannot reproduce.
