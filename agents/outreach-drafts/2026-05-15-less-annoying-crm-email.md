Subject: Seedstrap era and the team side of the AI question

Tyler,

The Seedstrap era essay is one of the more thoughtful operator-side takes on AI I have read this year. Family-owned since 2009, $5.4M ARR, 100K users in 70 countries, and the 2026 roadmap shipping board view and rich text instead of an AI bolt-on is consistent with the framing. The product is less annoying, not less considered.

The reason I am writing is the second half of the Seedstrap idea. You named AI as tooling for small teams to compete with VC-funded peers. That framing applies to your own operation as much as it applies to your customers. HubSpot ships Breeze, Salesforce ships Agentforce. They have 200 people building it. You have a family team running the same playbook at higher margins.

The audit I run with founders in this position is the team side, not the product side. Where does AI take 8 to 12 hours a week off Less Annoying's internal operation. Support triage, churn analysis, content QA, billing exceptions. None of it touches the brand promise of human-built simplicity.

Worth 20 minutes to compare notes on the team side specifically?
