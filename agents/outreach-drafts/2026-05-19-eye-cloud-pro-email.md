Hello,

Eye Cloud Pro does something hard well. EHR, POS, inventory, scheduling, and clinical charting in one place for 500+ private practices, with a 900+ member community behind it.

The strength is also where the friction lives. An optometrist moves through one patient workflow that touches several systems, and clinical charting is still largely manual. That is the strongest case for AI that acts inside the workflow rather than a feature added to one screen. Charting that drafts itself, inventory that updates from the exam, a POS step that does not need re-keying.
