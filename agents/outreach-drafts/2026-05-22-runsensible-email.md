Kaven,

The top seven legal practice tools all shipped AI in the last 18 months, and RunSensible looks AI-empty from the outside. That gap is real, but copying Clio Duo's bolt-on assistant would waste your actual differentiator.

Your edge is all-in-one across litigation, immigration, real estate, and PI in a single system. The incumbents are mostly point-solutions with AI stapled on. That breadth is exactly what AI can exploit. An assistant that works across a firm's whole matter, intake to billing to court forms, instead of one that helps with a single task, is something a single-feature competitor structurally cannot match. Your PDF court-form autofill is already a hint of that cross-workflow strength.

The move is to make AI ride your breadth, not to bolt on a narrow chatbot to look current.

I run a practice that maps where AI fits a product's real structure for vertical SaaS at $500K to $5M ARR.

Worth 20 minutes to compare notes on RunSensible specifically?
