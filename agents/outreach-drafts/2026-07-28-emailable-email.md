Subject: SOC2, and the question a per-address check does not answer

Sean,

Emailable's SOC2 posture is doing more competitive work right now than the AI language on the site is. Verification itself is a checkbox. NeverBounce, ZeroBounce, Bouncer and Kickbox all ship the same API call.

The question none of them answer well is the one senders actually have. Will this campaign hurt my sending domain. That is a longitudinal reputation problem across everything you have already scored, not a per-address decision. AI-drafted outbound is about to multiply how many lists need that answer, and from buyers who are systems rather than marketers.
