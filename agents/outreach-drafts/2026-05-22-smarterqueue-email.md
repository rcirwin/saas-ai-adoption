You have said you have followed AI progress for over a decade, so you will see the trap clearly. AI caption generation, the feature you and every competitor shipped, is now commoditized. Predis.ai and Ocoya are AI-native and will keep that arms race expensive and undifferentiated.

Your actual differentiator is content recycling. That is where AI gets genuinely interesting and where almost no one else can follow, because they do not have the recycling DNA. Not 'write me a caption' but AI that knows which evergreen post to resurface, when, reframed for which audience and platform, based on what has actually performed. Recycling intelligence, not caption generation. That deepens the one thing that already makes SmarterQueue worth choosing over Buffer.

Put AI where you are unique, skip it where everyone is the same.
