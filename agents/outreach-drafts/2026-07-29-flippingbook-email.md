Andrei,

Twenty-five years bootstrapped and 50,000 companies means FlippingBook already owns the thing most tools never earn, which is being where the sales collateral actually lives.

The shift I would watch sits outside the editor, on the reading side. A buyer handed a 40-page catalog increasingly asks an assistant to read it and answer three questions, and a flipbook is built to be looked at rather than queried. That makes the document structure underneath, and your tracking data about what got opened, the more interesting asset. More so than adding AI translation because Issuu did.

I run a fixed-scope assessment for B2B SaaS at $500K to $5M ARR, 8 to 12 weeks, four deliverables. It starts with workflow research, not feature ideation.
