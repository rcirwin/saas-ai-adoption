Ethan,

You and Jonathan built ConvertFlow out of agency experience, which means the product reflects how a smart marketer thinks. Build the rules, branch by traffic source, optimize. That logic is exactly what Mutiny attacked when it ate the high-end B2B market with AI deciding personalization in real time instead of a human authoring rules.

The 'AI Funnel Builder' label is on the site, but the engine is still mostly rules. The defensible move is to genuinely close that gap. Not adding an AI feature beside the builder, but letting AI decide what each visitor sees based on everything you know, while the marketer sets goals instead of branches. That is the shift from configuring funnels to running them.

Your no-code, SMB-friendly position is a real advantage if you make AI decisioning accessible, which is exactly where Mutiny is expensive and enterprise.

I run a practice that maps that shift for B2B SaaS at $500K to $5M ARR.
