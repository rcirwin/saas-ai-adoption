Reva,

Your HealthIQ analytics already personalize toward a student's health goals, which puts Lessonbee ahead of most K-12 tools that treat personalization as a nice-to-have. It also makes the AI question harder for you than for a generic LMS, because the subject matter carries real risk.

That is exactly why I think the interesting design work here is drawing the line before the tech, not after. Where generative AI helps a teacher adapt pacing or reframe a lesson, and where it must never sit anywhere near sensitive health guidance. Most vendors ship the chatbot and reason about safety later. Your product is the case for the opposite order.
