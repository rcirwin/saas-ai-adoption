Subject: what changed when lead routing got the sequence right

Mehdi,

The pattern in lead management: the calling agent goes first. Aktify did it on the LeadConduit side and much of the category followed. The trouble is that everyone rents the same model, so it lands as parity inside a year.

What does not commoditize is the decision the caller executes. Which buyer gets this lead, at what price, under what exclusivity window, and what happens when two sources send the same person twice. That routing logic is what Ohmylead already owns pre-CRM, and it is exactly what an AI caller has to be told before it dials.

So my read is the waitlist is worth shipping, but the durable version treats calling as an output of the routing decision, not a feature beside it.
