Subject: AI Guru shipped, the creator workflow underneath it

Shankar,

AI Guru shows Learnyst isn't sitting still. The pattern I keep seeing in course platforms, though, is that the durable AI win isn't a student-facing tutor. Teachable and Thinkific all shipped one. It's redesigning the creator's actual workflow: course structuring, assessment generation, the parts an institute spends real hours on.

For a platform that just grew 113 percent, the risk is bolting features onto a stretched product. The higher-return move is picking the one creator workflow where AI removes the most manual work and rebuilding it around that.

I run a fixed-scope assessment for B2B SaaS at $500K to $5M ARR, 8 to 12 weeks, four deliverables. It starts with workflow research, not feature ideation.
