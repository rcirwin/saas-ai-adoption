Matt,

Twelve years in, you have watched Stripe Smart Retries quietly absorb the basic version of what dunning used to charge for, while FlyCode plants the AI-native flag. The squeeze is real, and matching FlyCode on AI marketing is not the answer.

Your edge was never just retry timing. It was understanding why a payment fails and what to do about that specific customer. That is where AI actually belongs. Reading the decline reason, the customer's history, and the lifecycle stage to choose the recovery path, then handling the cancel flow as a real conversation rather than a fixed sequence. AI pointed at recovered revenue, not at a homepage badge.

When the commodity floor rises, you win by going deeper into the part that is hard, not louder on the part that is now free.

I run a practice that maps where AI fits a workflow for B2B SaaS at $500K to $5M ARR, and where it does not.
