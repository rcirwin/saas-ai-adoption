Aleem,

The CRM category spent last year shipping the same three things. Auto data capture, natural language questions about deals, a summary of the pipeline. Streak has the first two, so that race is finished for you.

What none of them shipped is the part that only works with history. Attio can stand up an assistant in a quarter. It cannot stand up fourteen years of watching where a deal actually lives, which is in a reply thread and not in a stage field.

You have described running Streak like a bootstrapped product even with the YC label on it. That constraint is the useful part. It means one AI bet, not five, and the one worth arguing about is whether the inbox record becomes something an agent reads from rather than a place a person files into.

Happy to share the directional read on where I would start, no commitment. Open to a short call or just trading notes here.
