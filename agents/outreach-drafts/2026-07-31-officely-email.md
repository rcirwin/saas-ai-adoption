Subject: Productivity Insights, and the step after it

Max,

Productivity Insights answers what happened. Who came in, which days clustered, how the space got used. That is the right first build, and it is where most workplace tools stop.

The step after it is a decision, not a report. Which day should this team co-locate next week, given who is on what project and who has already booked. Officely is unusual in that it already lives in Slack and Teams, which is where that suggestion would land anyway. Envoy and Robin have to pull people into a separate app to do the same thing.

So the question is whether desk booking stays a form someone fills in, or becomes something that proposes, then books.

I run a fixed-scope assessment for B2B SaaS at $500K to $5M ARR, 8 to 12 weeks, four deliverables. It starts with workflow research, not feature ideation.

Worth a 20-minute call to compare notes on where that research would start for Officely?
