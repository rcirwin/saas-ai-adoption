Subject: Ruler's AI Agent, answers vs another dashboard

Ian,

Ruler going from a 2009 PPC agency to $3M ARR in marketing measurement is a strong arc, and the AI Analyst and Media Planner agent in your hero shows where you are heading.

The question that decides whether that agent matters: does it act on attribution, or describe it? Attribution has always been data-heavy and hard to make actionable. Marketers do not want a smarter dashboard. They want the agent to recommend the budget move and, eventually, make it. That is a workflow, not a chat box on top of charts.

I run a practice that helps B2B SaaS teams figure out which AI features actually change user behavior. For a data-heavy product, the data-readiness assessment is usually where the agent's real ceiling shows up.
