Subject: Almost died twice, and the Blotato question

Ervin,

Reading the "$0 to $3M almost died twice" thread, the per-channel pricing wins from Buffer customers, and the $5M next milestone post is one of the cleanest bootstrapped operator-public stories on X. 100K+ brands at 15 people is a hard run.

The reason I am writing is what Blotato is doing on the other side. VC-backed, AI-native, rebuilding the scheduling workflow agent-first rather than feature-first. AI Assist and AI Image Generator are reasonable Publer features but they sit on top of a scheduling product that still works like scheduling worked in 2019. Blotato's bet is that the entire UX changes when AI is the primary interface, not a sidebar tool.

The audit I run with bootstrapped founders facing AI-native disruption is the rebuild question. What does Publer look like if you assumed Blotato's bet is right. Where does the analytics depth and per-channel pricing actually defend, and where does the scheduling UX need to be torn up. That is product strategy work, not feature roadmap work.

Worth 20 minutes to compare notes before the $5M push?
