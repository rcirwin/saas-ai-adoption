Danny,

Shipping Discovery AI in November was the right reflex. An assistant that answers questions about visitor behavior is a solid v1. The trouble is that Microsoft Clarity gives away AI summaries for free, and FullStory built AI in from the start. Summarizing behavior is becoming the floor, not the edge.

The differentiated move is AI that acts on the behavior, not just describes it. Spotting a rage-click pattern and proposing the fix, flagging the funnel step that quietly broke this week, triggering the survey to the exact segment that is churning. That turns analytics from a thing people read into a thing that does something.

With a heavy free-tier base, picking the AI that justifies the paid tier matters more than shipping more summaries.

I run a practice that maps which AI moves earn their place for B2B SaaS at $500K to $5M ARR.

Worth 20 minutes to compare notes on Lucky Orange specifically?
