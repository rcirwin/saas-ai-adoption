Ten years of building a 200 million record database out of Ukraine, through the last four of those, is the part of the GetProspect story that does not get said enough.

The thing I would be protective of is that a contact database is a decaying asset. Every record is a bet that someone still holds the job they held when it was captured, and the AI sequencing on top only pays off when that bet is right. Apollo and Clay are converging on finding. Recency is the harder square because it is an operations problem rather than a model problem, and it is the one buyers notice inside a week.
