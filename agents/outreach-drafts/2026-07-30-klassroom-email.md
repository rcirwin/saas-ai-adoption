Frank-David,

Klassroom carries children's data across thirty countries, which makes the AI decision narrower for you than it is for ClassDojo. They can roll something out in one market and iterate. You are choosing under GDPR plus whatever each national education authority says about minors.

The read I would offer is that the constraint works better as the design brief than as the blocker. Message drafting and translation can run without a model ever holding a child's record, if that boundary gets drawn on purpose at the start. Teams that draw it afterwards either rebuild it or quietly ship something they cannot defend to a school board.
