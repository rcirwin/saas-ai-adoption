Subject: Three record months without product AI is the story

Marko,

Three consecutive record months Feb to April, plus the 100% compliance score from the 12 EU privacy auditors. Most of the analytics category is in feature-race mode and you are quietly growing fastest by doing the opposite. That is the story.

The reason I am writing is what you are not doing. You are tracking inbound AI traffic with the new dashboard, but you have kept generative AI out of Plausible itself. That is a defensible position. Privacy and "ChatGPT in your analytics" are not naturally compatible, and the brand opinion is the moat.

So the question is not whether to add AI features. It is whether AI can save your team time on the inside without showing up to customers. Support triage, billing exception review, content workflows, abuse detection. None of it touches the privacy promise.

That is the audit I run with bootstrapped, opinionated teams in your range. It is a different conversation than the one Hotjar or PostHog would have.

Worth 20 minutes to compare notes? Happy to share patterns I am seeing with similar teams first.
