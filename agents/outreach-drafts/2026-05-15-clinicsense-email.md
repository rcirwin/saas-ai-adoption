Subject: No-Show Guard and the AI scribe question

Daniel,

No-Show Guard is the right kind of feature for a 14-year vertical SaaS. Rule-based, narrow, measurable. Practitioners feel the result on day one. The competitor AI features in the broader healthcare-PM space are mostly noisier.

The shift that's been quietly real is AI scribes for treatment notes. Heidi and Freed are eating practitioner time-savings in adjacent verticals (physio, allied health, primary care). For massage and wellness, the equivalent (soap-note assist, intake form auto-fill, no-show prediction beyond the Guard's rule logic) is open territory. None of those are sexy AI marketing. All of them shift the practitioner's actual day.

I run a fixed-scope assessment for B2B SaaS at $500K to $5M ARR, 8 to 12 weeks. The work maps workflow friction first, then sequences which AI bets earn their place. For ClinicSense the conversation is which two AI moves compound on top of the automation depth you already have, not which AI feature to copy from Jane App or Cliniko.
