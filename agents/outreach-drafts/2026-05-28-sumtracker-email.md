Subject: Inventory AI is moving marketplace-side, not tool-side

Kush,

The inventory AI conversation has shifted in the last six months. Shopify Magic, Amazon's seller AI, Walmart's Sparky. The reorder, restock, and listing decisions are starting to get made on the marketplace side and pushed down to sellers, rather than made inside an inventory tool.

The bet that compounds for Sumtracker is not another forecasting feature. It is being the layer that translates between three or four marketplace AIs that each think they own the seller's reorder logic. That is a hard, specific, narrow problem. And it is one that Shopify Magic structurally cannot solve, because by definition Shopify Magic only sees the Shopify slice.

The trade is interesting given a 9-person bootstrapped team. Worth a 20-minute call to compare notes on where I would start the workflow research for that translation-layer thesis?
