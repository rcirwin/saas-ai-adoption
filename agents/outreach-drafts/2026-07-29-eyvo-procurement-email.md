Sachin,

In procurement the AI squares funded players win are the model-spend squares: spend classification, invoice matching, RFQ scoring. Coupa and Ramp will outspend anyone there and they know it.

The square capital does not buy is the approval and policy layer. Who is allowed to commit money, under which threshold, with what evidence attached, and what happens when the answer is unclear. That is where mid-market buyers actually stall, and it is the one place nobody yet trusts a model on its own.

Eyvo has run bootstrapped in exactly that seat since 2012, which is why I think the second question matters more for you than the first, even with the AI features already shipped.
