Subject: 1.7B emails, and where AI actually pays

Ashraful,

Running 59,000 businesses and 1.7B emails sent on a team of six is a genuinely efficient operation, and in a category where everyone is bolting AI, I would not chase feature parity with Mailchimp.

For a low-cost bulk sender, the AI that pays is narrow and unglamorous: deliverability scoring, list hygiene, and subject-line lift, the places where a few points of inbox placement is real money for your customers. The opening is picking the one or two AI moves with clear ROI, not a generative assistant that adds cost you would have to pass on.

I run a fixed-scope assessment for B2B SaaS at $500K to $5M ARR, 8 to 12 weeks, four deliverables. It starts with workflow research, not feature ideation.
