Othmane,

Two billion email queries is not a vanity number. It is a decade of knowing which patterns resolve and which bounce, which is a different asset from the finder itself.

Here is why I am writing now. The Chrome extension assumes a human is looking at a LinkedIn profile. Clay and Apollo are being built on the assumption that a sequence, not a person, asks for the address, and that shift moves the buying decision from interface quality to whether the endpoint answers reliably at volume. Finding is commoditizing fast. Verification accuracy on a corpus that size is the part nobody copies in a quarter, and it is worth deciding whether it is a feature of your UI or a product of its own.
