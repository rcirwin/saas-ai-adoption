Subject: Referral category in the agent era

Dinesh,

30K brands without taking the AI bait Yotpo and LoyaltyLion are chasing is a real decision, not an oversight. Worth thinking through what comes next.

The shift I keep watching in commerce SaaS is that referral, loyalty, and customer journey are stacking under platforms with deeper AI. The bigger question is one layer up. As AI shopping assistants start mediating which brands consumers consider, referral becomes a question about agent discoverability, not just human virality. Stanley, Magical, PD Paola got there through humans sharing links. The next wave gets there through agents recommending brands, and the referral tooling has to be readable to those agents.
