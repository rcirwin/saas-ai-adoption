Subject: Data-native is the precondition for real AI

Chris,

Vero's data-native positioning is one of the more honest reads on what makes email marketing actually work. Customer.io and Iterable have raised heavy and they're winning the AI marketing narrative right now, but the part they're not saying out loud is that AI-personalized triggers don't work without the data layer to feed them.

That's Vero's argument and it's the one I think is undersold right now. "Data-native is the precondition for real AI" is a stronger positioning beat than "we also have AI." The trick is naming where AI compounds on the data stack you already let customers control, vs replacing it.
