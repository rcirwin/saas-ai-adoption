Subject: When tutorial creation becomes one click

Sundeep,

30M+ learners and 7,000+ customers over a decade is a serious adoption footprint. The original iorad insight (capture once, embed everywhere) was right, and the engagement analytics layer is what made it a platform vs. a tool.

What I am writing about is what happens when creation goes to one click. Scribe auto-captures, Supademo auto-generates, Tango ships AI captions, Glitter AI and Guidde are AI-native. The creation step is becoming a commodity feature inside 12 months. The interesting question is not how iorad matches Scribe on capture AI. It is what AI does for adoption, which is iorad's actual differentiator.

Adoption is harder to autogenerate. Knowing which tutorial a specific learner needs, when to surface it, how to detect failure mid-workflow, how to learn from 30M learner sessions to redesign which steps are tutorial-worthy in the first place. That is the workflow AI nobody in the category has built.

That audit is what I run with founders in categories being autogenerated. Worth 20 minutes?
