Subject: what changed when a KM tool got the agent sequence right

Daniella,

The knowledge-management tools pulling ahead with AI didn't start with a chatbot over their docs. They started with structure: making sure the underlying procedures were modeled cleanly enough that an agent could traverse them and act, not just retrieve a paragraph. Most KM content is too flat for that. The retrieval is only as good as the map.

ProcedureFlow is unusual here. You already built hyperlinked visual process maps for compliance teams, which is closer to an agent-traversable graph than the wikis Stonly and Guru are now bolting AI onto. That structure is the asset. The question is what an agent should be allowed to do with it in a compliance setting, which is its own design problem.

If useful, I can share the directional read on where I'd start with ProcedureFlow, no commitment. Open to a short call or just trading notes here.
