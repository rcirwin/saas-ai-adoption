Across invoicing the first AI went to extraction and categorization, and that is table stakes now. FreshBooks, Xero and Bonsai all have it, and none of them win on it.

That is where the support hours go, and with a team of five it is the one bet that returns time rather than a feature bullet.

Invoice Ninja has seen more of those edge cases than most, because 200,000 businesses generate them in the open.
