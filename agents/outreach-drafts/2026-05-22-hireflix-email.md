Antonio,

You built Hireflix on doing one thing well, and 'easiest to use' is the whole point. So the wrong move would be racing HireVue and the AI-native entrants on a sprawling AI scoring suite. That breaks the exact thing your customers chose you for.

The right question is narrower and more in character. What is the one place AI removes work for a recruiter without adding a single decision they have to learn or distrust? Summarizing a stack of async interviews into a ranked shortlist with the reasons, so a hiring manager skips the rewatch. That respects simplicity and it respects the candidate, which the scoring-heavy tools struggle with.

Picking the AI that fits a focused product, and skipping the rest, is the entire game when simplicity is your moat.

I run a practice that maps that for B2B SaaS at $500K to $5M ARR.

Worth 20 minutes to compare notes on Hireflix specifically?
