Subject: Watermarks in the summarizer era

Bill,

Video hosting is the category label on Spotlightr. Encryption, dynamic watermarks and domain restriction are a control product, and course creators pay for control.

Which makes this moment stranger for you than it is for Wistia. Every tool that can transcribe and summarize an arbitrary video is, from a course seller's point of view, a leak. If the lesson can be compressed into a paragraph by the buyer's own assistant, the thing worth protecting stops being the file and becomes the sequence, the practice and the assessment around it.

That is a positioning question before it is a feature question. The part I would want to know first is how many of your creators have already found a course of theirs summarized somewhere they did not put it.

I run a fixed-scope assessment for B2B SaaS at $500K to $5M ARR, 8 to 12 weeks, four deliverables. It starts with workflow research, not feature ideation.

Worth a 20-minute call to compare notes on where that research would start for Spotlightr?
