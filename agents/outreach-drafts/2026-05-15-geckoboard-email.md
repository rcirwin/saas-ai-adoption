Subject: 18 years of dashboards and the Copilot question

Paul,

I caught the 250th SaaS Club episode. The "failing 12 times before PMF" arc is one of the more honest founder stories in B2B. 18 years and 3.6K customers later, Geckoboard is the most well-iterated dashboard product in the SMB segment.

What I am writing about is the homepage and what is not on it. Tableau shipped Einstein, Power BI shipped Copilot, Klipfolio and Databox are racing on NLQ. The category narrative is quietly shifting from "shared visibility on a TV" to "ask the data and get an answer." Office Copilot makes the second one default for any team already in Microsoft.

That is not a "you need AI" pitch. The question is sharper. With 18 years of dashboard design knowledge, where does AI actually help your specific user (operations leads watching team KPIs on a screen) and where does NLQ make the dashboard irrelevant. That audit is what I do with bootstrapped founders in your category.

Worth 20 minutes to compare notes on what that looks like for Geckoboard specifically?
