Subject: Uku's vision statement, the roadmap underneath it

Rain,

Your published vision says AI handles the routine so accountants can focus on what matters. That's a real position. Karbon and Financial Cents have shipped their interpretations of the same line. Yours is still the cleanest articulation of the destination, but the roadmap to get there is the harder part.

As CPO that question is yours to answer. The trap is shipping AI features that look like Karbon's. The opportunity is figuring out which routine in the Estonian and broader European CAS firm workflow actually drains practitioner time, then mapping where AI moves it. That's the third interpretation and it's the one that compounds.

I run a fixed-scope assessment for B2B SaaS at $500K to $5M ARR, 8 to 12 weeks, four deliverables. The work starts with workflow research, not feature ideation. For a bootstrapped team with the unit economics Uku has, that order matters more than for the funded competitors.

Worth a 20-minute call to compare notes on where the workflow research would start?
