Subject: Usermaven + Maven as agent infrastructure

Waqar,

Maven AI exists. AI attribution exists. PostHog, Amplitude, Mixpanel all have their assistants. The interesting fight isn't feature parity.

What I keep watching in product analytics is that the AI assistants are all destinations right now. Users open the tool, ask the assistant, read the answer. That layer commoditizes fast. The next moat is whether the assistant becomes infrastructure that other agents call. A PM's planning agent queries Maven for funnel data while drafting a roadmap doc. A growth team's experiment agent pulls attribution context for a launch decision. The Maven AI does the analytical work without anyone opening Usermaven.

That positions Maven as analytics-agent infrastructure, not just an in-app feature. Different moat, much harder to copy.
