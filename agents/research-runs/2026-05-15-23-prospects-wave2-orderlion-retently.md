## New Memory Patterns from this Run

1. **YC W24 + founders from AI-deep backgrounds (Drift, Capital One AI, Amazon Robotics) = automatic fit 2.** Reform.app is the textbook case. When founders have built conversational AI for $B+ companies, they will build AI in-house rather than buy a $30K assessment. Memory should flag 'founder-prior-AI-role at FAANG/Drift/Stripe/etc' as a downgrade signal independent of YC-grad-devtool pattern.

2. **Pantastic Networks added to SaaS holding-co disqualifier list.** Confirms memory's pattern: when a SaaS company has been acquired by a portfolio rollup (SureSwift, Constellation Software, Tiny Capital, Awesome Motive, Saas.group, Pantastic Networks), founder is gone or now-operator, no FRS conversation. Always check Crunchbase Acquisitions tab when researching.

3. **Resend MCP server is the clearest 'category-leader-agent-ready' disqualifier of this batch.** They expose discovery at /.well-known/mcp.json, ship Agent Skills, market 'agent-experience' as a blog category, and have 'agent email inbox' as a product. When the company has standardized on the MCP discovery convention, they're not just bolt-on agent-ready — they're defining the category convention. Fast disqualifier.

4. **Procuros confirms VC-growth-mode disqualifier at the high end of ICP.** $5.3M ARR + $9M Seed from top-tier (Point Nine + Creandum + Snowflake CMO/Workday CTO/Adyen co-founder as angels) = the company is in execute-the-VC-thesis mode, not strategic-pause mode. Pattern: when angel investors include senior execs from category-leader SaaS (Snowflake, Workday, Adyen), the company has already done the strategic-positioning work that FRS would replicate.

5. **Multi-front operations platforms with bolt-on AI per pillar = clean fit 4.** Outvio consolidates fulfillment + shipping + returns + helpdesk + 3PL. Each pillar has its own AI-native competitor (Gorgias for helpdesk, Loop for returns, etc) and Outvio has thin AI per pillar.
