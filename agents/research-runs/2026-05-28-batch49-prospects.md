Strong outreach hook ("operator-built for operators") and credibility.

- **"Holding-co rebuild" rebrand pattern**: Apoorva-rebuilds-6Storage exactly mirrors Constellation/SureSwift/Cordance behavior. When a 'technology investment firm' announces ground-up rebuild + new CEO + new version, treat as acquisition disqualifier even if not described as one.
- **Founder-also-runs-an-AI-native-product is interesting**: Damon Chen runs Testimonial.to (no AI) + PDF.ai (AI-native). The founder *understands* AI strategy deeply — they've just deliberately or by-default kept Testimonial AI-light. Strong angle for an assessment: he can act on findings fast.
- **CEO contradiction signal**: 6Storage sheet listed founder/CEO blank; current Michelle Otto is post-Apoorva-partnership CEO (typical holding-co playbook = bring in new operating exec). If sheet shows blank founder + current CEO Crunchbase has no co-founder tag, check Crunchbase for "joined as CEO" pattern.
- **'AI Powered' badge ≠ shipped AI**: The Customer Factor lists 'AI Powered' on Cuspera but no actual AI features found in product. Always verify the AI claim against product pages, not directory badges.
- **`forms.app` M&A offer (April 2025, not closed) = yellow flag**: Reconfirms memory pattern. ConvertCalculator was same — yellow flag, fit 3 with caveat.
- **CRM-inside-Outlook = existentially threatened by Microsoft Copilot**: Avidian/Avid CRM is in ICP but the existential threat is Microsoft Copilot for Sales absorbing the Outlook surface, not absence of AI. Score 3 — assessment angle has to be "how to coexist with Copilot as agent surface."
