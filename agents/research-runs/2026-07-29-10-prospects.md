## Flagged issues

### BLOCKER — every prospect website was unreachable (egress policy)

**All 10 company websites returned HTTP 403 on CONNECT through the agent proxy.** Per `/root/.ccr/README.md`, a 403/407 means the destination host is not permitted by this session's egress policy; the README explicitly says not to retry or route around it. Blocked hosts: `stonegridusa.com`, `assignr.com`, `backflow.com`, `bakesmart.com`, `curbsidelaundries.com`, `cybake.com`, `ecosconnect.com`, `escaperoommaster.com`, `ezshred.com`, `golfoclock.com`.

Third-party hosts (SourceForge, Capterra listings via search, trade press, directory profiles) were reachable, and web search returned substantive content. **All 10 scores therefore rest on secondary sources only** — `sources_checked` records `website-403(egress-blocked)` for each. Consequence: the "no AI features observed" findings are weaker than usual, because the primary place a vendor announces AI is its own site. Treat `none`/`exploring` postures in this run as provisional; a re-check with website access could move a score in either direction. Recommend the operator whitelists prospect domains for the researcher's egress policy before the next run.
