## Top 5 by fit score

### 1. `fastbound` — fit 4 — FastBound (FFL/ATF firearms compliance)
**Angle:** data-readiness + competitive moat. Sixteen years of structured A&D / 4473 / serial-chain compliance data across many POS stacks, used only to *record* compliance and never to *predict* inspection exposure. Lead on inspection-exposure scoring and A&D anomaly detection; second beat is the timing objection (no AI-native rival exists in FFL compliance yet).
**Why strong:** Confirmed ~$3.9M revenue (in band), bootstrapped with zero funding across 16 years, ~20 staff, named co-founder Jarad Haselton with authentic founder-market fit (former FFL dealer). Clean `none` posture. Clears every cap `fflboss` was held at 3 on in the 2026-07-24 run.

### 2. `actionflow` — fit 4 — ActionFlow (countertop / stone fabrication)
**Angle:** workflow-first. "Your workflow builder is the right instinct, but every rule in it still has to be authored by a human who already knows the answer." Move from deterministic trigger-and-action rules to adaptive orchestration (remake prediction, slab yield, quote-to-close scoring).
**Why strong:** In-band 1M–3M, very high workflow complexity (quote → template → slab nesting → CNC/DXF → fabrication → install → multi-location), clean `none` posture, and the buyer already believes automation creates value — shortest possible distance to an agentic blueprint. Named technical founder Reese Watt with a strong origin story.

### 3. `routegenie` — fit 4 — RouteGenie (NEMT / medical transport)
**Angle:** competitive-moat + the "we already have AI features" objection. Contrast Tobi Cloud's AI-first dispatch claim against the gap between RouteGenie's own AI blog post and its shipped feature set, then frame the 2.5M-trips/month dataset as an unmined moat.
**Why strong:** In-band ARR, very high regulated workflow (eligibility → multi-load dispatch → Medicaid broker claims), `bolt-on` posture that is mostly marketing language over classical optimization. New search-fund CEO Cameron Craig (acquired 2025) with a growth mandate is a high-intent strategy buyer.

### 4. `roverpass` — fit 3 — RoverPass (RV park / campground PMS)
**Angle:** workflow-first / "AI features ≠ the right ones." They have publicly bet AI on shipping speed and marketing. Open by asking what AI does for the park owner at 7am on a Saturday, then anchor on dynamic pricing and occupancy forecasting as the moat Campspot takes first.

### 5. `rx4route` — fit 3 — Rx4Route (pharmacy last-mile logistics)
**Angle:** data-readiness with an "AI features ≠ the right ones" opener. They claim "AI-powered" in press but ship route optimization; lead on what the delivery dataset could actually predict (failed-delivery risk, refill timing and adherence, controlled-substance compliance exposure).

Also researched at fit 3: `yardsmart` (self-service auto-recycling yard management — clean `none` posture in a legacy-desktop category, elite founder reachability, capped by unknown/sub-floor ARR and split founder attention).
