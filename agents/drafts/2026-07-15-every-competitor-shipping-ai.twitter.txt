1/ Every competitor in your space shipped an AI feature last quarter. You didn't.

That feels like falling behind. Usually it isn't.

---

2/ Look at what most of those features actually are.

A chat box bolted onto a screen.
A "summarize" button.
A suggestion the user still has to go verify.

Shipping fast looks like progress. In the market it's noise until it changes what the product does.

---

3/ The panic move: copy the feature so you're not the only one without it.

The clarity move: ask what job your users are trying to finish, and whether any of those features finish it.

---

4/ The buyer's question is shifting.

From "can this help my team do X?" to "does this just do X?"

A me-too AI feature loses on both. It barely helps, and it doesn't do the job.

---

5/ I'd take one feature that completes a workflow end to end in six months over five that suggest and summarize by Friday.

Shipping the wrong thing isn't neutral. It burns engineering time, a roadmap slot, and board credibility.

---

You're not behind for skipping the feature everyone copied. You're behind if you can't name the job your product should own.
