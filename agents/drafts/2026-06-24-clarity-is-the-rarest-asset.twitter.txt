1/ The scarcest thing in B2B SaaS right now isn't AI talent or budget.

It's clarity.

---

2/ Most founders I talk to aren't stuck because they can't build.

They're stuck because they can't tell which bet is real.

---

3/ The pressure is everywhere. The board wants an AI story. Competitors are shipping. The team keeps sending demos of the latest model.

Underneath it all: which of these actually matters for our product?

---

4/ So the default move is to ship something.

A chat box. A "summarize" button. "AI-powered" in the release notes.

It feels like progress. It buys a quarter of cover.

---

5/ But shipping motion isn't shipping a bet.

A feature that doesn't sit on real workflow friction doesn't move retention. It just adds surface area to maintain, and a label that's starting to read as a red flag.

---

6/ The founders who feel calm aren't moving fastest. They got clear first:

- Named the one workflow where AI changes the economics, not the ten where it's cosmetic.
- Said out loud what they're NOT building this year.
- Can name what an agent couldn't route around.

---

7/ Clarity is rarer than speed because speed is cheap now.

Anyone can ship an AI feature in a weekend. Knowing which one is worth owning end-to-end is the hard part. That's the part that compounds.
