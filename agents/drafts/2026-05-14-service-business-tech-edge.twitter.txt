1/ Service based businesses are going to start looking more like tech companies than tech companies do.

For 20 years, software was on a pedestal. Huge margins. Huge multiples. Massive ability to scale.

🧵

---

2/ Agents may have just inverted who has the harder problem.

Tech companies built the tools. Software margins, software multiples, software exits.

But the tool was always a means. The customer was buying the outcome.

---

3/ Service businesses owned the outcome from day one.

Trial-and-error reps. Judgment from hundreds of cases. The workflow. The customer relationship.

What they didn't have was the leverage. Margins capped by hours in the day.

---

4/ Agentic harnesses change the math.

Embed your domain knowledge into a harness that runs the workflow, and you approach software margins without giving up outcome ownership.

Software-like leverage. Services-like depth.

---

5/ I've seen founders scrap their AI SaaS and pivot to running the workflow as a service.

They own all the inputs and outputs. Unlocks larger budgets. Deals close faster.

The agent is the leverage. The service is the product.

---

6/ Three things follow that most operators are missing.

The edge. The judgment agentic harnesses need lives in the heads of operators who have run the workflow thousands of times. Not in the SaaS founders just building the tools they use.

---

7/ The bottleneck. The agent's real bottleneck is domain context.

Domain context doesn't exist in training data. It exists in the operator who already knows what the right answer looks like.

---

8/ The scramble. Software companies built for tool delivery now have to figure out how to deliver outcomes.

Going from outcomes to leverage is a far shorter trip than going the other way.

Services don't realize the goldmine they're sitting on.

10/ For two decades, scale meant becoming a software business.

That path is closing. The new path is to stay a service business and let the agent provide the leverage.

---

11/ The question isn't whether your service-based business can become a tech company.
