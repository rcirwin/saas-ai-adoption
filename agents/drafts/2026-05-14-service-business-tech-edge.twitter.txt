1/ Service businesses are about to look more like tech companies than tech companies do.

For 20 years, the playbook said: stop being a service business, become a software business.

Agents just flipped which one has the harder problem.

🧵

---

2/ Tech companies built the tools. Software margins, software multiples, software exits.

But the tool was always a means. The customer was buying the outcome.

---

3/ Service businesses owned the outcome from day one.

Trial-and-error reps. Judgment across hundreds of cases. The workflow. The customer relationship.

What they didn't have was the leverage.

---

4/ Agentic harnesses change the math.

Embed your domain knowledge into a harness that runs the workflow, and you approach software margins without giving up outcome ownership.

Software-like leverage. Services-like depth.

---

5/ A founder friend just scrapped his AI SaaS to become a service business that runs the workflow his customers were trying to do inside it.

Owns all the inputs and outputs. Margins climbing. Deals closing faster.

The agent is the leverage. The service is the product.

---

6/ Three things most operators are missing.

The edge. The judgment agentic harnesses need lives in the heads of operators who have run the workflow thousands of times. Not in the SaaS founder shipping the first version of it.

---

7/ The bottleneck. The agent's real bottleneck is domain context.

Domain context doesn't exist in training data. It exists in the operator who already knows what the right answer looks like.

---

8/ The scramble. Software companies built for tool delivery now have to figure out how to deliver outcomes.

Going from outcomes to leverage is a far shorter trip than going from leverage to outcomes.

---

9/ The model is the engine. The harness is the product.

The people best positioned to build the right harness are the ones who already know what right looks like.
