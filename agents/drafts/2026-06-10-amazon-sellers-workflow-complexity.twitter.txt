1/ I spent years building software for Amazon sellers.

The smartest ones never used the feature we were proudest of.

They kept a spreadsheet instead.

---

2/ We'd ship a clean dashboard for a decision they made every week. Repricing, restock timing, which SKUs to kill.

Power sellers would nod, say it was nice, then go back to a spreadsheet with twelve tabs and a macro nobody else could read.

---

3/ I thought it was a UX problem. Build it cleaner, they'll switch.

Then I mapped what was actually in the spreadsheet.

It wasn't data. It was judgment.

---

4/ Seasonality learned the hard way. A supplier who slips every Chinese New Year. The keyword that cannibalizes another. Refund patterns that mean a listing is about to get suppressed.

Our dashboard answered the easy 80%. The spreadsheet carried the 20% that moved money.

---

5/ That 20% lived in their head. No tool ever captured it, because the expert never wrote it down.

A model can do the 80%. So can your dashboard.
