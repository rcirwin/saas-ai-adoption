1/ You don't need an AI hire yet.

You need a plan.

---

2/ I keep watching SaaS teams post the same job: "Senior AI Engineer."

Six figures. Three months to fill. A month to onboard.

Then they hand that person a blank page and a board that wants AI by Q3.

---

3/ A hire solves how to build.

It does nothing for what to build.

And the expensive mistakes almost always live in the what.

---

4/ The pattern I keep seeing:

The roadmap is a wishlist of features that demo well, not workflows that hurt.

---

5/ The data the model needs is fragmented across three systems nobody mapped.

---

6/ The judgment that would make the AI useful is locked in two people's heads, undocumented.

---

7/ None of that gets fixed by a stronger engineer.

The bottleneck isn't model intelligence or talent.

---

8/ It's the absence of a plan: which workflow to own, what context the model needs, where the human judgment lives.

---

9/ Hire first, and you pay someone six figures to discover all of this on the job.

10/ I'm not saying never hire. I'm saying sequence it.

The best AI hires I've seen executed a strategy that already existed. They didn't invent one under board pressure.

---

11/ If your AI roadmap is still a list of features instead of a plan for owning a workflow, what would your new hire actually build in week one?
