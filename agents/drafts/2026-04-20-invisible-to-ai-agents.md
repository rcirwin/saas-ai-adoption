Your product is invisible to AI agents right now.

Not because the agents are bad. Because your product was built for a human with a mouse.

I've been pulling apart B2B SaaS products from the agent's point of view, and the same gaps show up almost every time:

– Your auth flow assumes a person clicking through a browser.
– Your "API" covers maybe 40% of what your UI can do.
– Your most useful actions live behind modals, multi-step wizards, or buttons with no programmatic equivalent.
– Your docs describe endpoints, not jobs-to-be-done.
– Your data is shaped for dashboards, not for an agent trying to make a decision in one call.

When an agent shows up on behalf of your user, it can't see most of your value. So it routes around you. It uses the tool that exposes the workflow cleanly, even if that tool is objectively worse for a human.

This is one of many signals, but it's a loud one: the products winning early agent traffic aren't always the best products. They're the most legible ones.

The fix isn't an "AI feature." It's structural. Treat agents as a real user persona. Map the workflows you'd want them to run end-to-end. Then ask the uncomfortable question — can they actually do that today, without a human in the loop?

If the honest answer is no, that's the gap to close before a competitor does.

What's one workflow in your product an agent couldn't complete today, even if it tried?
