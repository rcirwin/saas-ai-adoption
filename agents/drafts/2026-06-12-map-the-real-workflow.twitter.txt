1/ The workflow your users describe is not the workflow they actually run.

The version they walk you through is the clean one. The one that fits the demo.

The real one lives in the gaps between your features.

---

2/ The real workflow is the spreadsheet they export to.

The Slack message to double-check a number.

The screenshot pasted into a doc.

The step they do at 11pm because the tool can't.

---

3/ You will never find any of it by asking "how do you use the product?"

You have to map the workflow underneath the workflow.

Four questions get you there.

---

4/ 1. Where do you leave the product to get this done?

The export, the copy-paste, the second tab.

That's where the job actually continues.

---

5/ 2. What do you check before you trust this number?

The double-check reveals the real decision they're making.

And the anxiety attached to it.

---

6/ 3. What part do you redo every single time?

Repeated manual work is the highest-leverage friction.

It's also where users have stopped expecting better.

---

7/ 4. What did you do right before you opened the tool, and right after you closed it?

The job starts before your product and ends after it.

Most of the pain lives in those edges.

---

8/ The point isn't to add AI to the steps inside your product.

It's to find the steps your product made the user do somewhere else, and complete them.
