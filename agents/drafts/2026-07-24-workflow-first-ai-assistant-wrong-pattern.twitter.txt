1/ The "AI assistant" is the most popular AI pattern in B2B SaaS.

It's also usually the wrong one.

---

2/ Not because assistants are bad.

Because of what the word "assistant" quietly commits you to.

---

3/ An assistant suggests.

It drafts, recommends, surfaces, then hands the work back to your user to finish.

The human still completes the job.

---

4/ I've watched a few teams ship the assistant version of a feature. The pattern rhymes every time:

The sidebar suggests. The user still does the task.
The chat answers. The user still decides.
The "AI draft" gets rewritten by hand anyway.

---

5/ Usage spikes in week one. Then it settles into "nice to have."

Retention doesn't move.

---

6/ A suggestion is one of seven steps in the workflow.

You're still only owning that one.

---

7/ Wrong question: "where can we add an assistant?"

Right question: "which job can we complete end to end and hand back finished?"

---

8/ That's the line between a tool and a service.

An assistant keeps the user driving, and charges per seat.

Completing the job delivers an outcome, and can charge for it.
