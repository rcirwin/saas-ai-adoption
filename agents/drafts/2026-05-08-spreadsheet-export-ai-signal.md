The strongest signal of where to put AI in your product isn't in your roadmap doc.

It's in your CSV export logs.

Every time a user clicks "Export to spreadsheet," they're telling you something specific: your product stopped being useful at that step, and they went somewhere else to finish the job.

I've watched this in product after product. Amazon sellers exporting keyword data to pivot it in Excel. Ops teams pulling reports out so they can join them with data your tool doesn't know about. PMs exporting the dashboard so they can write the actual narrative in a Google Doc.

The export is the white flag.

What users are actually doing on the other side of that export is the AI feature. It's almost always one of three jobs:

- Joining your data with something you don't have
- Reshaping it for a decision you don't model
- Writing the summary, recommendation, or next step you don't generate

That's where the workflow is breaking. That's where AI belongs. Not on the dashboard you already shipped, with a "Summarize" button that paraphrases what they're already looking at.

The order matters. Find the export. Watch what happens after it. Build the AI feature that makes the export unnecessary.

Workflow first. AI second.

What does your top user do in the 10 minutes after they hit "Export"?
