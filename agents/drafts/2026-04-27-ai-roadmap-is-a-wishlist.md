Your AI roadmap is a wishlist, not a strategy.

It's a list of features your competitors shipped, sorted by which one your CEO saw on LinkedIn most recently.

I've been looking at AI roadmaps from B2B SaaS teams in the $500K-$5M ARR range. The same gaps show up:

- No tie to a specific user workflow
- No call on which decisions AI should make vs. surface
- No view on how external agents will reach the product
- No order beyond "ship the demo-friendly one first"

That's not a strategy. That's an internal Slack thread with bullet points.

A real AI strategy answers four questions before any feature gets a ticket:

1. What painful, repeated workflow are we removing?
2. What decision are we making easier - and for whom?
3. What does our data need to look like to make that work?
4. How does an external agent interact with this, if at all?

If a roadmap item can't answer those, it's a wishlist item. Ship it and you'll get a press release and flat retention.

Workflow first. AI second.

What's on your AI roadmap right now that can't survive those four questions?
