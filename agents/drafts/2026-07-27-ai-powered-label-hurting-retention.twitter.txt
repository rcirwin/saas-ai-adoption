1/ The "AI-powered" badge on your site is doing more damage to your retention than the feature behind it.

Not because the AI is bad. Because of the promise the label makes for you.

---

2/ A buyer who reads "AI-powered" doesn't hear "we added a model."

They hear "this does the work."

---

3/ That becomes the bar renewal gets measured against a year later.

Almost nobody sets that bar on purpose.

---

4/ The promise.

You shipped a summarizer, a suggestion panel, a smart field in settings. The buyer read the badge and expected the job to come back finished.

---

5/ At renewal they aren't comparing your product to last year's version.

They're comparing it to what the badge implied.

---

6/ The comparison set.

The label moves you out of your own category. Say "AI-powered" and you stop getting benchmarked against the other three vendors on the shortlist.

---

7/ You start getting benchmarked against the contractor or agency the buyer would have hired instead.

Per-seat pricing looks strange inside that comparison. You're charging for access while implying you deliver a result.

---

8/ The signal.

Powered. Enabled. Assisted. Augmented.

Every one is a modifier. Every modifier confirms a human still does the work here.

---

9/ Buyers have started reading them that way.

The badge that was supposed to say "we're ahead" increasingly reads as "we're a tool with a model bolted on."

---

10/ None of this says hide the AI.

It says the label is downstream of the decision, not a substitute for making it.
