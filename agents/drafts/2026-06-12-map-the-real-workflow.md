The workflow your users describe is not the workflow they actually run.

Ask them to walk you through their process and you get the clean version. The one that fits the demo. The one that lives in the product.

The real workflow lives in the gaps between your features.

It's the spreadsheet they export to. The Slack message they send to double-check a number. The screenshot they paste into a doc. The step they do at 11pm because the tool can't.

You will never find those by asking "how do you use the product?"

**The 4 questions I ask to map the workflow underneath the workflow:**

1. Where do you leave the product to get this done? The export, the copy-paste, the second tab. That's where the job actually continues.

2. What do you check before you trust this number? The double-check reveals the decision they're really making, and the anxiety attached to it.

3. What part do you redo every single time? Repeated manual work is the highest-leverage friction. It's also where users have stopped expecting better.

4. What did you do right before you opened the tool, and right after you closed it? The job starts before your product and ends after it. Most of the pain lives in those edges.

The point isn't to add AI to the steps inside your product.

It's to find the steps your product made the user do somewhere else, and complete them.

**Workflow first. AI second. The map is the whole game.**

Which of these four questions would change what's on your roadmap if you actually asked it this week?
