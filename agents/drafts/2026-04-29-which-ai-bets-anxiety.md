A SaaS founder told me last week he hasn't slept properly in a month.

Not because of churn. Not because of runway.

Because every Monday a new competitor ships an AI feature, and he doesn't know if the one his team is building is the right one.

I've heard a version of this from five founders in the last 60 days. Same shape every time:

- Board is asking what the AI plan is
- Team has three half-built ideas
- Nobody can say with a straight face why those three
- The founder is the one carrying the doubt

The anxiety isn't about AI. It's about making a bet you can't reverse with a team that's already stretched.

Here's the part that surprised me. The founders sleeping the worst aren't the ones doing nothing. They're the ones shipping fastest. Speed without a thesis just compounds the doubt - every release becomes evidence that maybe you picked wrong.

The founders who seem calm have something boring in common. They can finish this sentence in one line: "We're betting on AI that removes ___ for ___ , because ___."

Not a roadmap. A thesis. One sentence the whole team can repeat back.

If you can't write that sentence today, the answer probably isn't another sprint. It's a week of not shipping while you figure out what you actually believe.

What's the one sentence you'd put on the whiteboard for your team this week?
