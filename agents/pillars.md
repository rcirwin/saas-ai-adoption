# Content Pillars

Six pillars. Each has an `id` (used by commands), a core thesis, and 5-10 angles to draw from.

Pick one pillar per post. If user provides `angle`, use it. If not, pick a strong unused angle from the list.

---

## 1. `ai-agent-thesis`
**Thesis**: SaaS built for humans gets routed around by AI agents. Agent-readiness is a product architecture question, not a feature question.

**Angles**:
- Why your product is invisible to AI agents today
- The difference between "adding AI" and becoming agent-ready
- How agents will choose which SaaS tools their users interact with
- Why API quality decides who wins the agent era
- The window to define how agents interact with your category is closing
- What "agent-native" actually means (vs. marketing language)
- Why vertical SaaS is more exposed than horizontal
- The data surface your agents need vs. what you expose today
- What changes when agents, not humans, are your primary user

---

## 2. `workflow-first`
**Thesis**: Most AI features fail because they start from technology, not user workflows. The question is never "where can we add AI?" It's "what's painful every time?"

**Angles**:
- The 3 questions to ask before building any AI feature
- Where users currently export to a spreadsheet = where AI should live
- Why feature-first AI loses to workflow-first AI
- How to map a user's real workflow (it's never what you think)
- The difference between a suggestion and an action
- Why "AI assistant" is usually the wrong pattern
- Signs your AI feature is solving the wrong problem
- How to identify high-leverage workflow friction
- Why workflows beat features, every time

---

## 3. `pm-lessons`
**Thesis**: Stories and anonymized lessons from Jungle Scout, Data Dive, Scale Insights. Real PM experience from data-heavy, workflow-intense B2B SaaS.

**Angles**:
- A pattern I saw across 100+ user interviews
- What Amazon sellers taught me about workflow complexity
- Why the feature users ask for isn't the feature they need
- The retention insight that changed how I build products
- A launch that failed and what I learned
- How I run user interviews that reveal real friction
- What scale insights taught me about automation
- Data I used to prioritize (and the data I ignored)
- The one question that unlocks a user interview
- When "users want this" is the wrong signal

---

## 4. `contrarian-takes`
**Thesis**: Sharp, opinionated claims that attract exactly the right buyers and repel the wrong ones. Polarizing on purpose.

**Angles**:
- Most SaaS AI features are solving the wrong problem
- Your AI roadmap is a wishlist, not a strategy
- "Add AI to everything" is why your retention is flat
- Chatbots are the wrong answer to most product questions
- You don't need an AI hire yet — you need a plan
- Adding a chat interface is not an AI strategy
- Why generic AI roadmap decks are a scam
- Stop building for AI demos and start building for users
- The $0 AI feature that beats every paid one
- Why your "AI-powered" label is hurting retention

---

## 5. `founders-dilemma`
**Thesis**: Speak to the emotional state of the buyer — the anxiety of not knowing what to build, the pressure from boards, the feeling of falling behind.

**Angles**:
- The anxiety of not knowing which AI bets to make
- How to tell the board you're not shipping AI yet
- What to do when every competitor is shipping AI
- The pressure of a generational platform shift
- How to resist pressure to build the wrong thing
- When shipping nothing beats shipping the wrong thing
- What to say to your team when AI feels overwhelming
- The cost of a bad AI first move (higher than you think)
- Why clarity is the rarest asset right now
- How to tell an investor you're doing strategy, not shipping

---

## 6. `behind-the-process`
**Thesis**: Show what the assessment actually looks like. Teardowns, frameworks, examples of deliverables. Builds trust without pitching.

**Angles**:
- What a workflow audit actually uncovers
- What a data readiness assessment looks like
- The 4 deliverables and why each one exists
- A framework for evaluating AI feature bets
- How to spot a workflow that's ready for agents
- The "agent readiness" scorecard (simplified)
- What 8-12 weeks of assessment actually includes
- How I write an AI blueprint (structure + example)
- The questions I ask on a discovery call
- What happens after the assessment ships

## Cadence Guidance

If the Content Planner is choosing a pillar for a given day, suggested rotation:

| Day | Pillar (default) |
|-----|------------------|
| Mon | `ai-agent-thesis` or `contrarian-takes` |
| Wed | `pm-lessons` or `founders-dilemma` |
| Fri | `workflow-first` or `behind-the-process` |
| Sat (optional) | `contrarian-takes` (engagement driver) |

Rotate so no pillar runs 2 days in a row.
