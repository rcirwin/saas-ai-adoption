50 net-new added covering 12 distinct marketplaces. Run completed 2026-05-28. Sheet went 489->539 for my batch (parallel sourcers ran concurrently, sheet at 653 total).

**Why:** Caller's "marketplace residents" angle - established small-team vendors riding inside major B2B SaaS platforms. Strict scope: not standalone vertical SaaS, not standalone horizontal SaaS, not geographic indie backlists.

**How to apply:** When sourcing marketplace-resident vendors in the future:

1. **Atlassian Marketplace is heavily PE-rolled-up via Appfire** ($100M TA Associates + Silversmith). Appfire owns Botron, Wittified, Bob Swift Atlassian Apps, BigPicture/SoftwarePlant, Comalatech, Spartez, Anova Apps, Feed Three. Tempo rolled up ALM Works (2021), Old Street Solutions, Amovos. GitKraken owns BigBrassBand. Mindville acquired by Atlassian 2020. Riada acquired by Eficode. DEISER acquired by Devoteam Jan 2026. Always run `[vendor] Appfire OR Tempo OR Atlassian OR acquired` before deep-qualifying. See [[atlassian-marketplace-rollups]] cluster.

2. **WordPress plugin ecosystem dominated by Awesome Motive (WPBeginner Growth Fund) + StellarWP (Liquid Web) + WPMU DEV + Brainstorm Force**. True indie survivors are rare: Saturday Drive (Ninja Forms), WPManageNinja (Fluent Forms), WP Fusion (Very Good Plugins), FORSBERG+two. BuddyBoss acquired by Awesome Motive Jul 2024 (latest casualty).

3. **Vietnamese Shopify app ecosystem rich**: OmegaTheme, Avada, GemPages, PageFly, Beae, Globo, Joy Loyalty all bootstrapped/lightly funded, 15-100 emp, $1-10M ARR. Common pricing $10-99/mo SaaS on Shopify. PageFly/GemPages/Avada/OmegaTheme already at upper-cap edge.

4. **Vietnamese pattern + Bangladesh pattern**: WPManageNinja (Sylhet Bangladesh) follows similar pattern — multi-product (Fluent Forms + FluentCRM + FluentBoards + FluentCart + FluentPlayer + Ninja Tables), 115 emp, profitable, founder-led (Shahjahan Jewel).

5. **GetLatka stale-snapshot trap again**: Plauti latest snapshot ($1M ARR) didn't reflect Aug 2024 Super Round Robin acquisition. Standuply went DOWN from $600K 2021 to $154K 2023 — disruption signal real.

6. **AI positioning is fastest DQ**: Relume, Slater, Anima, Synder, Squivr AI, BoosterAI all DQ'd. Slater rebrand by Edgar Allan as "AI-powered code env" turned a fit into a skip. Always grep marketing copy first.

7. **Founder-led + small angel round is OK**: Common Ninja $1.2M raise, Loop Subscriptions Klaviyo angel passed. Distinguish from Bunny.net ($5.88M Series A from Runa Capital) which crossed line.

8. **Two main bootstrapped Atlassian clusters surfaced**:
   - Eastern European: codefortynine (Germany), HeroCoders (Poland), SaaSJet (Ukraine), Stiltsoft (Estonia), eazyBI (Latvia), SoftComply (Estonia), Caelor (Croatia), Mibex (Switzerland), Plerdy (Ukraine), Actonic (Stuttgart)
   - Antipodean: Easy Agile (Wollongong AU), ProjectBalm (Menai NSW AU), Aprika (Melbourne AU), Highway Three (Victoria BC Canada)

9. **Family-owned + multi-decade WP/Shopify shops underpriced as a vein**:
   - FORSBERG+two (Bjorn + wife Sarah, Copenhagen since 2010)
   - Saturday Drive (Kevin Stover + James Laws Tennessee + coffee + email)
   - Marsello (Brent Spicer NZ since 2014)
   - Lightward (Isaac Bowen + husband Abe Lopez, Locksmith since 2014)

10. **Female-founded Atlassian marketplace vendors are extremely rare** — Highway Three (Karen Fishwick founder, 2001) is uniquely positioned for warm-intro / differentiation hook.

11. **Notion ecosystem template-makers vs SaaS-makers**: Templates are $0K-$50K micro-MRR (Tiago Forte, Marie Poulin) — below ICP floor. SaaS apps for Notion API are the right fit (NotionForms, Whalesync though Whalesync DQ'd for YC).

12. **Pipedrive Marketplace is under-explored**: Outfunnel (Andrus Purde ex-Pipedrive marketing head) is the textbook fit. Likely more ex-Pipedrive employees building Pipedrive-native apps — search "ex-Pipedrive founder" for next batch.

13. **Stripe Apps marketplace is competitive AI-native space**: Lemon Squeezy acquired by Stripe Jul 2024. Most new entries are AI-native (Creem, Dodo Payments). Existing indies like Synder went YC. Limited fits.

14. **GitHub Marketplace not deeply sampled this run** — opportunity for next batch.

15. **Quality bar held**: Refused to fill quota with sub-fit candidates. Sub-floor ARR ($154K-$270K) wrote when team fit + AI exposure clean (Standuply, NotionForms). Above-cap ARR ($5-10M) wrote with explicit "flag to researcher" notes (Easy Agile, K15t, Stiltsoft, eazyBI, WPManageNinja, OmegaTheme, DevSamurai, POWR).
