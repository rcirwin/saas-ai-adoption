# AI-native rebrand wave in MENA/LATAM/Africa 2025-2026

**Rule:** When sourcing in 2025-2026 MENA, Saudi-Vision-2030-funded, or recent-VC LATAM ecosystems, expect ~60% of "promising B2B SaaS" candidates to have rebranded as "AI-powered" / "AI-driven" / "AI-native" in their tagline or company name. These are auto-DQ per ICP rule 7.

**Why:** The AI hype cycle hit MENA particularly hard in 2024-2025 with Vision 2030 capital flowing to anything with "AI" branding. Most rebranded SaaS will market themselves as AI-native even when their underlying product is the same workflow tool from 2018. This is also showing up in LATAM where regional VCs (Cobre, Treble) pushed AI-first positioning to raise larger rounds.

**Confirmed DQ examples seen 2026-05-28:**
- **Sadq** (Saudi) — "AI-powered digital signature platform" — was just a digital signature company at launch 2022
- **Penny Software** (Saudi) — "AI-driven procurement platform" — was originally just procurement automation
- **Mozn** (Saudi) — RegTech AI — always AI-positioned but doubled down
- **Alaan** (UAE) — "AI-powered finance platform" — was just expense management
- **Colocio AI** (South Africa) — Was just "Colocio" — rebranded as "Agentic Marketing AI"
- **Treble.ai** (Colombia) — Heavy AI positioning post-Tiger funding
- **Klipy** (Korea, but small) — "AI based CRM platform"
- **Cero.ai** (Chile) — was healthcare communication, now ".ai" domain + AI-first
- **Genomawork** (Chile) — "AI-powered recruitment" — original was just recruitment
- **Tautona AI** (South Africa) — Built AI-first

**How to apply:**
1. Read the company tagline FIRST before any other diligence — if it leads with "AI-powered" / "AI-driven" / "AI-native" / "AI-first", auto-DQ unless their *original* product (Wayback Machine 2020-2022) had no AI element.
2. Domain ending in `.ai` is a strong DQ signal in new sources (post-2022).
3. Acceptable: companies that have AI *features* but lead with their domain function ("ERP for SMBs", "Field service software", "Inventory management") — these are workflow companies that added AI, not AI-natives.
4. Pre-2023 founding date + under $5M ARR + AI-feature-not-AI-positioning = clean ICP fit.
