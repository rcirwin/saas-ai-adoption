50-prospect run focused on content/asset/media management (PIM, DAM, MAM, headless CMS, video hosting, transcription, translation, image CDN, document automation, brand portals, proofing/approval, digital signage, virtual tour SaaS).

**Why:** Future runs targeting this territory should know the productive veins (Netherlands DAM, Australia/NZ proofing, Vancouver/UK bootstrapped video, Romania/Czech/Poland localization, UK/US virtual tour) and skip the now-known consolidated/acquired plays.

**How to apply:**
- **Richest veins:** (1) Netherlands DAM — Storyteq, Marvia, Pics.io tightly clustered bootstrapped $1-4M. (2) UK/AU proofing — Filestage, PageProof, Aproove, Better Proposals, GoVisually, Boords. (3) Bootstrapped indie translation — Locize, Tolgee (Czech), SimpleLocalize (Poland), Trustmary (Finland) all <$2M. (4) Virtual tour SaaS is a fresh under-mined sub-category — CloudPano, EyeSpy360, Kuula, Lapentor, Panoee all bootstrapped at $1-3M. (5) Video hosting bootstrapped indies: SproutVideo (NYC bootstrapped since 2010), Vidalytics, Spotlightr, Castr, Cincopa, Berrycast all fit ICP.
- **DAM consolidated:** Bynder, Brandfolder (Smartsheet), Acquia DAM (Widen), Aprimo (PE), Censhare (Germany 280+ emp), MediaValet (Toronto $13.7M), Wedia ($14M, French), Storyteq is the rare bootstrapped DAM Leader on Gartner MQ 2025.
- **PIM consolidated:** Akeneo (French unicorn), Salsify (mega), Plytix (now $20.5M + $12M raised), Pimberly (Series B $17.8M Manchester), Pimcore (Austria, Open Source + commercial), Sales Layer ($11.8M + €3.5M Series A). Apimio (Karachi/Toronto $3.2M bootstrapped) and PIMworks (smaller) are the rare independents.
- **Headless CMS consolidated:** Contentful (mega), Sanity ($85M+ raise), Storyblok ($138M raised), Strapi ($45M raised + $10M ARR), Hygraph, ButterCMS (acquired Tiugo). Statamic ($1.5M, 3 emp Jack McDade), Cosmic (YC W19 but small), Crystallize (Norway $2M Sebastian Gjertsen), Squidex (Germany Sebastian Stehle solo dev) are independents.
- **Tiugo Technologies** is the new multi-product SaaS holding to watch — owns ButterCMS (Dec 2022), Uploadcare (Jan 2024), CKEditor, TinyMCE. Future runs: auto-DQ any candidate where parent is Tiugo.
- **saas.group** is the other major multi-product holding ($50M ARR across 16 brands incl. Usersnap, Mailtrap, others). Auto-DQ.
- **AcceptedTacit category:** even though "graphic design" is excluded per mandate, *brand-template-with-DAM* tools (Marq formerly Lucidpress, BrandKit, Marvia, Storyteq) fit because the asset library + workflow is the product, not the design surface.
